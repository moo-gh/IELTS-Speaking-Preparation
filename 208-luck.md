#### 1. Do you believe in luck?
**Partly**—**random** **events** **clearly** **shape** **life**, but I **don't** **rely** on **luck** **instead** of **preparation**. **Hard** **work** **creates** **more** **chances** for **luck** to **matter**, **especially** in **exams** and **applications**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever had a lucky experience?
**Yes**—**missing** a **bus** that **broke** **down** **later**, **finding** a **lost** **wallet** **returned**, or **getting** a **last**-**minute** **spot** on a **course** I **needed**. **Small** **lucky** **moments** **happen**, but I **try** not to **build** **decisions** around them.

---
#### 3. Do people in your country believe in superstitions?
**Some** **do**, **especially** **older** **generations**—**lucky** **numbers**, **avoiding** **certain** **days**, or **rituals** before **travel** and **weddings**. **Younger** **people** **joke** about **superstitions** **more**, but **traditions** **still** **appear** at **family** **events**.

---
#### 4. Are there any lucky numbers or symbols in your culture?
**Yes**—**specific** **numbers** **linked** to **prosperity** or **harm**, plus **symbols** like **eyes**, **coins**, or **animals** **used** as **charms**. **Not** **everyone** **takes** them **seriously**, but **gifts** and **decorations** **often** **follow** these **ideas**.

---
#### 5. Do you think success comes from luck or hard work?
**Mostly** **hard** **work**, **timing**, and **support**, with **luck** **adjusting** **small** **edges** at **critical** **moments**. **Attributing** **everything** to **luck** **excuses** **lazy** **habits**; **ignoring** **luck** **completely** **ignores** **privilege** and **random** **breaks**.

---
#### 6. Would you describe yourself as a lucky person?
**Average**, **I'd** **say**—**enough** **good** **surprises** to **feel** **grateful**, **enough** **bad** **luck** to **stay** **humble**. **Overall** I **trust** **effort** **more** than **waiting** for **fortune** to **rescue** me.
