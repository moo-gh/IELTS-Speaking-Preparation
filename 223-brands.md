#### 1. Do you pay attention to brands when you shop?
**Sometimes**—for **items** that **last** a **long** **time**, like **shoes** or **electronics**, I **check** **reputation** and **reviews**. For **basic** **groceries** or **stationery**, the **brand** **matters** **less** than **price** and **quality**.

---
#### 2. Do you prefer famous brands?
**Not** **always**—**well**-**known** **names** can **mean** **reliable** **service**, but they **often** **cost** **more** for the **logo** **alone**. If a **less** **famous** **product** **works** **just** as **well**, I'm **happy** to **save** **money**.

---
#### 3. Why do people buy expensive brands?
**Status**, **trust**, and **habit**—some **people** want **others** to **notice** what they **wear** or **drive**; **others** **believe** **premium** **brands** **last** **longer** or **feel** **safer**. **Advertising** and **influencers** **push** that **image** **constantly**.

---
#### 4. What kinds of brands are popular in your country?
**Global** **tech** and **fashion** **labels** in **cities**, plus **strong** **local** **food**, **clothing**, and **home** **appliance** **brands** **people** **grew** **up** **with**. **Car** **brands** and **phone** **makers** **get** **a** **lot** of **discussion** **online**.

---
#### 5. Have you ever bought something mainly because of the brand?
**Yes**, **once** or **twice**—**headphones** and a **backpack** where I **hoped** **better** **build** **quality** would **justify** the **price**. **Sometimes** it **did**; **sometimes** I **realised** a **cheaper** **option** would have been **fine**.

---
#### 6. Do you think young people care more about brands than older people?
**Often** **yes**—**social** **media** **rewards** **visible** **labels**, and **peers** **notice** **what** you **wear** at **school** or **university**. **Older** **people** may **focus** more on **comfort**, **durability**, and **value**, though **tastes** **vary** **widely**.
