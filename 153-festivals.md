#### 1. Do you like festivals?
**Yes**, **mostly**—they **break** **routine**, **bring** **people** **together**, and **give** **days** a **clear** **sense** of **meaning** beyond **work** and **study**. **Overcrowded** **events** can **feel** **stressful**, but **well**-**planned** **celebrations** **usually** **lift** my **mood**.

---
#### 2. What is your favourite festival in your country?
**New** **Year** and **major** **cultural** **holidays** **rank** **high**—**family** **visits**, **special** **food**, and **a** **shared** **feeling** that **everyone** is **marking** the **same** **moment**. **Smaller** **local** **festivals** **appeal** **too** when they **highlight** **music**, **crafts**, or **regional** **traditions**.

---
#### 3. How do people celebrate festivals where you live?
**Family** **gatherings**, **special** **meals**, **decorations**, **gifts**, **visiting** **relatives**, and **sometimes** **public** **events** in **streets** or **parks**. **Younger** **people** **also** **meet** **friends**, **watch** **fireworks**, or **travel** **briefly** when they **have** **time** **off**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer traditional or modern festival celebrations?
**A** **mix**—**traditional** **food** and **rituals** **feel** **rooted**, but **modern** **conveniences** like **online** **invites** and **flexible** **schedules** **make** **life** **easier**. I **dislike** **celebrations** that **feel** **purely** **commercial** **without** **real** **connection**.

---
#### 5. Have you ever taken part in a festival in another country?
**Not** **yet**, though I've **seen** **videos** and **heard** **friends** **describe** **events** like **carnivals**, **harvest** **festivals**, and **religious** **holidays** **abroad**. I'd **love** to **experience** one **in** **person** when **travel** **plans** **allow**.

---
#### 6. Do you think festivals are important for culture?
**Very**—they **pass** **stories**, **values**, and **shared** **memories** across **generations**, even when **daily** **life** **changes** **fast**. **Without** **festivals**, **communities** **risk** **losing** **rhythm** and **identity** **beyond** **shopping** and **screens**.
