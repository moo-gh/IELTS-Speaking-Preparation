#### 1. Are you a punctual person?
**Usually**, **yes**—I **hate** **rushing** and **prefer** **arriving** **five** to **ten** **minutes** **early** for **classes**, **meetings**, and **appointments**. **Traffic** or **oversleeping** can **throw** me **off**, but I **plan** **buffers** when **something** **really** **matters**.

---
#### 2. Do you think it's important to be on time?
**Yes**—**lateness** **wastes** **other** **people's** **time**, **creates** **stress**, and **signals** **disrespect** in **formal** **settings** like **interviews** or **exams**. **Occasional** **delays** **happen**, but **habitual** **lateness** **damages** **trust** **quickly**.

---
#### 3. How do you feel when others are late?
**Annoyed** at **first**, **especially** if they **didn't** **message** or **offer** a **reason**. If **delays** are **rare** and **honest**, I **stay** **calm**; if **someone** **always** **runs** **late**, I **start** **planning** **around** it **instead** of **pretending** it **won't** **happen**.

---
#### 4. Have you ever been late for something important?
**Yes**—**once** for a **group** **presentation** because **public** **transport** **broke** **down**, and **another** **time** I **misread** a **start** **time** on an **invitation**. **Both** **taught** me to **double**-**check** **details** and **leave** **earlier** than I **think** I **need**.

---
#### 5. What do you do to make sure you are on time?
**Set** **alarms**, **prepare** **bags** the **night** **before**, **check** **maps** for **travel** **time**, and **avoid** **starting** **long** **tasks** **right** **before** I **should** **leave**. **Simple** **habits** **beat** **panic** **sprints** at the **door**.

---
#### 6. Is punctuality important in your country?
**In** **work** and **formal** **education**, **yes**—**being** **late** to **class** or **meetings** **gets** **noticed** **fast**. **Social** **events** can be **more** **flexible**, **especially** at **parties**, but **professional** **settings** **still** **reward** **people** who **respect** **the** **clock**.
