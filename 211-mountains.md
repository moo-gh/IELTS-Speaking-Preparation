#### 1. Do you like mountains?
**Yes**—**fresh** **air**, **wide** **views**, and **quiet** **paths** **feel** **more** **restful** than **busy** **city** **streets**. Even a **short** **trip** to the **hills** **helps** me **reset** after **weeks** of **indoor** **study**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever climbed a mountain?
**Not** a **serious** **peak**, but I've **hiked** **up** **local** **hills** and **one** **easy** **trail** with **friends**. **Proper** **mountaineering** **needs** **gear** and **training**, so I **stick** to **safer**, **marked** **routes**.

---
#### 3. Are there mountains in your country?
**Yes**—**several** **ranges** and **popular** **spots** for **weekends** and **holidays**. **Some** **areas** are **famous** for **snow** in **winter** and **green** **slopes** in **spring**, which **draws** **tourists** and **local** **hikers**.

---
#### 4. Would you like to live near mountains?
**For** a **while**, **yes**—**cooler** **air** and **outdoor** **space** **sound** **appealing**. **Long**-**term** I might **miss** **city** **services**, **jobs**, and **easy** **transport**, so a **nearby** **visit** could **beat** **full**-**time** **living** there.

---
#### 5. What do people usually do in the mountains?
**Hiking**, **picnics**, **photography**, **camping**, and in **some** **regions** **skiing** or **snowboarding**. **Families** **often** **go** for **day** **trips**, while **others** **stay** **overnight** in **cabins** or **small** **guesthouses**.

---
#### 6. Do you think mountains are good for tourism?
**Definitely**—they **offer** **scenery**, **adventure**, and **seasonal** **activities** that **cities** **can't** **match**. **Tourism** **needs** **careful** **management** **though**, so **paths**, **waste**, and **wildlife** **don't** get **damaged**.
