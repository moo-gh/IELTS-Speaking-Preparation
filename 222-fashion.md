#### 1. Are you interested in fashion?
**A** **little**—I **notice** **trends** on **social** **media** and in **shops**, but I **don't** **follow** **runway** **shows** or **fashion** **magazines** **seriously**. For me it's **more** about **looking** **neat** than **staying** **ahead** of **every** **new** **style**.

---
#### 2. Do you follow fashion trends?
**Loosely**—I **might** **try** **popular** **colours** or **cuts** if they **suit** me, but I **won't** **buy** something **uncomfortable** just because it's **trendy**. **Classic** **basics** **last** **longer** in my **wardrobe** than **fast** **fashion** **pieces**.

---
#### 3. Where do people learn about fashion in your country?
**Online** **influencers**, **TV** **series**, **shopping** **malls**, and **friends** **sharing** **outfit** **photos** are **main** **sources**. **Traditional** **media** still **matters** for **formal** **events** like **weddings**, where **dress** **codes** are **stricter**.

---
#### 4. Is fashion important for young people?
**Quite** **yes**—**clothes** **signal** **identity**, **confidence**, and **group** **belonging** at **school** and **university**. **Pressure** **exists** **too**; **not** **everyone** can **afford** **constant** **new** **looks**, which **creates** **stress** **sometimes**.

---
#### 5. Would you like to work in the fashion industry?
**Probably** **not**—the **creative** **side** is **appealing**, but the **pressure**, **long** **hours**, and **competition** **sound** **exhausting**. I **respect** **designers**, but my **path** **leans** toward **other** **fields**.

---
#### 6. How has fashion changed compared with the past?
**Faster** **cycles** and **more** **casual** **everyday** **dress**—**suits** **appear** **less** **often**, while **sportswear** and **streetwear** **dominate** **younger** **crowds**. **Sustainability** **talk** has **grown** **too**, though **cheap** **online** **shopping** **still** **drives** **waste**.
