#### 1. Do you often feel stressed?
**More** than I'd **like**, **especially** during **exam** **season** or when **deadlines** **pile** **up** **without** **clear** **priorities**. **Daily** **life** is **manageable** **most** **weeks**, but **sudden** **changes**—**missed** **buses**, **broken** **laptops**, **unexpected** **costs**—can **spike** **stress** **fast**.

---
#### 2. What makes you feel stressed?
**Time** **pressure**, **unclear** **expectations**, **group** **work** with **unequal** **effort**, and **waiting** for **important** **results** I **can't** **control**. **Social** **conflict** **stresses** me **too**, **even** **small** **arguments** that **linger** in my **head** **afterwards**.

---
#### 3. How do you deal with stress?
**Short** **walks**, **music**, **talking** to **friends**, **breaking** **tasks** into **smaller** **steps**, and **sleeping** **properly** when I **can**. **Scrolling** **social** **media** **sometimes** **feels** like **relief** but **often** **makes** **stress** **worse**, so I **try** to **limit** it.

---
#### 4. Do you think life is more stressful now than in the past?
**In** **some** **ways**, **yes**—**constant** **connectivity**, **job** **competition**, and **fast** **news** **cycles** **keep** **people** **alert** **all** **the** **time**. **Older** **generations** had **different** **pressures**—**economic** **hardship**, **less** **technology**—so **stress** **hasn't** **disappeared**, it **just** **changed** **shape**.

---
#### 5. Is stress a problem for young people in your country?
**Very** **much**—**study** **pressure**, **social** **comparison** **online**, **uncertain** **job** **markets**, and **high** **living** **costs** in **cities** **weigh** on **many** **students**. **Schools** and **universities** **talk** about **mental** **health** **more** **now**, but **support** **still** **feels** **uneven**.

---
#### 6. What do you do to relax when you are stressed?
**Make** **tea**, **stretch**, **watch** **something** **light**, or **meet** a **friend** for a **walk** **without** **phones**. **Best** **relief** comes when I **finish** **one** **small** **task** and **prove** to myself that **progress** is **still** **possible**.
