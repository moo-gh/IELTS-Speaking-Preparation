#### 1. Do you like looking at the sky?
**Yes**—**especially** at **sunrise**, **sunset**, or on **clear** **nights** when **stars** are **visible**. **Watching** the **sky** **slows** me **down** for a **minute** and **feels** **calmer** than **staring** at a **screen**.

---
#### 2. Do you prefer a clear sky or a cloudy sky?
**Clear** **skies** for **photos**, **walks**, and **travel**, but **cloudy** **days** can **feel** **softer** and **easier** on the **eyes**. **Dramatic** **cloud** **formations** are **interesting** **too**, as **long** as **rain** **doesn't** **ruin** **plans**.

---
#### 3. Have you ever watched the sky change colour?
**Many** **times**—**blues** **shifting** to **orange**, **pink**, and **purple** near **sunset**. Those **short** **windows** of **colour** are **easy** to **miss** if I'm **indoors**, so I **try** to **notice** them when I'm **outside**.

---
#### 4. Do people in your country enjoy sky-watching?
**Some** **do**—**especially** during **meteor** **showers**, **eclipses**, or **beautiful** **sunsets** **shared** on **social** **media**. **City** **lights** **limit** **star**-**gazing**, so **people** **often** **drive** **out** of **town** for a **better** **view**.

---
#### 5. Would you like to learn more about the sky or astronomy?
**Yes**, at a **basic** **level**—**identifying** **planets**, **constellations**, and **why** **weather** **patterns** **form**. I **don't** **need** **advanced** **physics**, just **enough** to **understand** what I'm **looking** at.

---
#### 6. Is the sky different in different seasons where you live?
**Definitely**—**summer** **skies** **look** **brighter** and **stay** **light** **longer**, while **winter** **brings** **greyer** **days** and **earlier** **darkness**. **Seasonal** **changes** in **colour**, **cloud**, and **daylight** **shape** **mood** and **outdoor** **routines**.
