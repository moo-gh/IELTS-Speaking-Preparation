#### 1. How often do you have your hair cut?
**Every** **four** to **six** **weeks**, **depending** on how **fast** it **grows** and whether I **need** a **neater** **look** for **events** or **photos**. If I'm **busy**, I **stretch** it **longer**, but **then** it **starts** to **feel** **untidy** and **harder** to **style**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever had a haircut you didn't like?
**Yes**, **once** or **twice**—**too** **short** on **top** or **not** **matching** what I **asked** for, **especially** when I **couldn't** **explain** **clearly** in **another** **language**. I **learned** to **bring** a **photo** **reference** and **confirm** **length** **before** the **scissors** **start**.

---
#### 3. Do you like going to the hairdresser?
**It's** **fine**, but I **don't** **love** **small** **talk** while **someone** **works** **around** my **head**. **Good** **barbers** or **stylists** **make** it **relaxing**; **rushed** **ones** **make** me **nervous** that the **result** will **look** **sloppy**.

---
#### 4. What kind of hairstyle do you have?
**Simple** and **low**-**maintenance**—**short** on the **sides**, **slightly** **longer** on **top**, **easy** to **wash** and **dry** **quickly** **before** **class** or **work**. I **avoid** **styles** that **need** **daily** **products** or **long** **mirror** **sessions**.

---
#### 5. Would you like to change your hair colour?
**Maybe** **subtle** **highlights** **once**, but **nothing** **extreme** like **bright** **blue** or **full** **bleach**. **Natural** **shades** **suit** me **better**, and **dyed** **hair** **seems** like **extra** **cost** and **upkeep** I **don't** **need** **right** **now**.

---
#### 6. Is hairstyle important in your country?
**To** **some** **extent**—**clean**, **neat** **hair** **signals** **professionalism** in **interviews** and **formal** **settings**, **especially** in **conservative** **workplaces**. **Younger** **people** **experiment** **more**, but **most** **still** **care** about **looking** **presentable** in **public**.
