#### 1. Do you like snow?
**Yes**—I **like** how it **makes** the **city** **look** **quieter** and **brighter**. **Playing** in it is **fun**, though **commuting** in **heavy** **snow** can be **stressful**.

---
#### 2. Does it snow in your country?
**In** **some** **regions** and **higher** **areas**, **yes**, **especially** in **winter**. Where I **live**, **snow** is **less** **common**, so it still **feels** **special** when it **falls**.

---
#### 3. Have you ever played in the snow?
**Yes**—I've **built** **snowmen**, had **snowball** **fights**, and **walked** through **fresh** **snow** with **friends**. Those **days** were **cold**, but they are among my **favourite** **winter** **memories**.

---
#### 4. Would you like to live in a snowy place?
**For** a **season** or a **holiday**, **yes**, but **not** **permanently**. I would **miss** **milder** **weather** and get **tired** of **icy** **roads** and **constant** **layering**.

---
#### 5. What do people usually do when it snows?
**Children** **play** **outside**, **adults** **clear** **paths**, and **many** **people** **stay** **indoors** with **hot** **drinks**. In **tourist** **areas**, **skiing** and **snowboarding** become **popular**.

---
#### 6. Do you think snow affects daily life?
**Definitely**—it can **delay** **transport**, **close** **schools**, and **make** **walking** **harder**. At the **same** **time**, it **slows** people **down** and can **create** a **calmer**, more **festive** **mood**.
