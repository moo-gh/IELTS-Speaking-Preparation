#### 1. What was your favourite subject at school?
**English** and **science**-**related** **classes**—**English** because **stories** and **debates** **felt** **alive**, **science** because **experiments** **showed** **ideas** **working** in **real** **life**. **Math** was **useful**, but **less** **exciting** **unless** the **teacher** **explained** **applications** **clearly**.

---
#### 2. What subject did you dislike at school?
**History** **dates** **sometimes** **bored** me when **lessons** **turned** into **memorising** **lists** **without** **context**. **Physical** **education** was **fine**, but **team** **sports** **days** **stressed** me when I **wasn't** **confident** with **ball** **skills**.

---
#### 3. Do you think some subjects are more important than others?
**Practically**, **literacy**, **math**, and **basic** **digital** **skills** **matter** **everywhere**, but **arts** and **music** **shape** **creativity** and **confidence** **too**. **Ranking** **subjects** **too** **harshly** **pushes** **students** to **ignore** **talents** that **could** **become** **careers** **later**.

---
#### 4. Would you like to study any subject again in the future?
**Yes**—**advanced** **English**, **psychology**, and **maybe** **economics** to **understand** **money** and **decision**-**making** **better**. I'd **also** **revisit** **physics** **concepts** **slowly** **now** that I **care** **more** about **how** **technology** **works**.

---
#### 5. Did you study science or arts subjects?
**Both**, but **leaned** **science** in **later** **years** because **university** **plans** **pointed** that **way**. I **still** **took** **literature** and **art** **options** when **schedules** **allowed**, so I **wouldn't** **feel** **one**-**sided**.

---
#### 6. Do students in your country enjoy studying?
**Mixed**—**some** **love** **learning**, **others** **treat** **school** as **pressure** **mainly** for **exams** and **rankings**. **Good** **teachers** and **flexible** **projects** **increase** **enjoyment**; **constant** **testing** **without** **support** **burns** **motivation** **fast**.
