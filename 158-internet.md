#### 1. How often do you use the internet?
**Daily**, **almost** **constantly** for **study**, **messages**, **news**, and **small** **tasks** like **checking** **schedules** or **paying** **bills**. **Without** it, my **routine** would **slow** down **dramatically**, **especially** during **exam** **season**.

---
#### 2. What do you usually use the internet for?
**Research**, **assignments**, **language** **practice**, **video** **calls**, **maps**, and **occasional** **entertainment** when I **need** a **break**. I **also** **use** it to **compare** **prices** and **book** **tickets** instead of **standing** in **long** **queues**.

---
#### 3. When did you start using the internet?
**Primary** or **early** **secondary** **school**, **mainly** for **simple** **searches** and **games** at **first**. **Real** **dependence** **grew** in **teen** **years** when **homework**, **social** **life**, and **applications** **moved** **online**.

---
#### 4. Do you think the internet is useful?
**Extremely**—**information**, **communication**, and **services** are **available** **faster** than **before**, **especially** for **students** and **remote** **work**. **Used** **carefully**, it **saves** **time**; **used** **carelessly**, it **eats** **attention**.

---
#### 5. What are the disadvantages of using the internet?
**Distraction**, **misinformation**, **privacy** **risks**, and **pressure** to **stay** **connected** **all** **the** **time**. **Long** **hours** **online** can **hurt** **sleep**, **posture**, and **face**-**to**-**face** **conversation** **skills** if you **don't** **set** **limits**.

---
#### 6. Do you prefer using the internet on your phone or on a computer?
**Computer** for **serious** **writing** and **long** **reading**; **phone** for **quick** **checks**, **messages**, and **on**-**the**-**go** **tasks**. **Ideally** I **combine** **both**, but **small** **screens** **make** **deep** **work** **harder** for me.
