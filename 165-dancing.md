#### 1. Do you like dancing?
**Yes**, **in** **small** **doses**—**casual** **moves** at **parties** or **when** a **song** I **love** **comes** on, **not** **serious** **stage** **performance**. I **enjoy** the **energy** and **social** **fun**, but I **don't** **think** of myself as a **natural** **dancer**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever learned to dance?
**A** **little**—**basic** **steps** at **school** **events** and **one** **short** **workshop** with **friends** **years** **ago**. **Formal** **training** **never** **lasted** **long** because **practice** **time** **competed** with **study** and **other** **hobbies**.

---
#### 3. What kind of dancing do people in your country like?
**Traditional** **folk** **dances** at **weddings** and **festivals**, plus **modern** **styles** like **hip**-**hop**, **salsa**, and **club** **dancing** among **younger** **people**. **TV** **talent** **shows** **boosted** **interest** in **structured** **dance** **too**, **especially** in **cities**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer watching dance or dancing yourself?
**Watching** **professionals**—**their** **timing**, **control**, and **storytelling** **through** **movement** **impress** me **more** than my **own** **attempts**. **Dancing** **myself** **feels** **better** when the **crowd** is **friendly** and **nobody** **judges** **mistakes** **harshly**.

---
#### 5. When was the last time you danced?
**At** a **friend's** **birthday** **gathering** **a** **few** **months** **ago**—**just** **light** **movement** to **music** in the **background**, **nothing** **choreographed**. **Before** that, **wedding** **parties** were the **main** **places** I **danced** **without** **feeling** **too** **self**-**conscious**.

---
#### 6. Is dancing popular in your country?
**Yes**, **especially** for **celebrations**—**weddings**, **national** **holidays**, and **cultural** **events** **almost** **always** **include** **music** and **dance**. **Fitness** **studios** **also** **offer** **dance** **classes** **now**, so it's **not** **limited** to **parties** **anymore**.
