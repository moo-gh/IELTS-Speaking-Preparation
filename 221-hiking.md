#### 1. Do you like hiking?
**Yes**, **when** the **weather** is **mild** and I have **enough** **energy**. I **enjoy** **fresh** **air**, **quiet** **paths**, and **seeing** **views** you **don't** get from a **car** **window**.

---
#### 2. How often do you go hiking?
**Not** **very** **often**—**maybe** **a** **few** **times** a **year** on **weekends** or **holidays**. **Study** and **work** **schedules** **limit** **long** **trips**, so **short** **walks** **near** **home** **count** as **hiking** for me **sometimes**.

---
#### 3. Where do people usually hike in your country?
**Mountain** **trails**, **forest** **parks**, and **coastal** **paths** are **popular**, **especially** in **cooler** **months**. **Some** **regions** have **well**-**marked** **routes** with **rest** **stops**; **others** **rely** on **local** **guides**.

---
#### 4. Who do you usually go hiking with?
**Mostly** **friends** or **family**—it's **safer** and **more** **fun** with **company**. **Solo** **hikes** **happen** **rarely**, and **only** on **easy**, **familiar** **trails** I **know** **well**.

---
#### 5. What should people take when they go hiking?
**Water**, **snacks**, **comfortable** **shoes**, **sun** **protection**, and a **basic** **first**-**aid** **kit**. A **map** or **offline** **GPS** **matters** **too**, **especially** **away** from **phone** **signal**.

---
#### 6. Is hiking popular among young people?
**Growing** **yes**—**social** **media** **photos** and **health** **trends** **encourage** it. **Not** **everyone** **enjoys** it; **some** **prefer** **gym** **workouts** or **city** **activities** **without** **long** **drives** to **trailheads**.
