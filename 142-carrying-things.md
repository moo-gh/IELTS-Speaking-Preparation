#### 1. When you go out, what do you usually carry with you?
**Phone**, **wallet**, **keys**, and a **small** **bag** with **earphones** and a **reusable** **bottle** on **most** **days**. **Study** **trips** add a **laptop** or **notebook**; **quick** **errands** I **strip** it **down** to **card** and **phone**.

---
#### 2. What do you do if something you need to carry is very heavy?
**Ask** for **help**, **split** the **load**, or **use** a **trolley** / **backpack** so **weight** **sits** on my **back**, not my **arms**. **Stubbornly** **dragging** **heavy** **bags** **alone** **usually** **ends** with **sore** **shoulders** and **slow** **walking**.

---
#### 3. Do you carry different things depending on where you are going?
**Definitely**—**campus** means **chargers** and **notes**; **gym** means **towel** and **change** of **clothes**; **cafés** with **friends** I **travel** **lighter**. **Weather** **changes** the **list** **too**—**umbrella** or **extra** **layer** when **forecasts** **look** **unreliable**.

---
#### 4. Is there a difference between what you carry in the morning and in the evening?
**Mornings** I **pack** for the **whole** **day**—**laptop**, **lunch**, **keys**—while **evenings** I **often** **leave** **heavy** **stuff** **behind** and **carry** **just** **essentials**. **Late** **walks** **feel** **easier** with **empty** **hands** and a **phone** in my **pocket**.

---
#### 5. Have you ever forgotten something important when you went out?
**Yes**—I've **left** my **charger** at **home** before **long** **library** **days**, and **once** **forgot** an **umbrella** in **sudden** **rain**. **Now** I **do** a **quick** **doorway** **check**: **phone**, **keys**, **wallet**, then **anything** the **day** **specifically** **needs**.

---
#### 6. Do you prefer carrying things in a bag or in your pockets?
**Bag** for **anything** **beyond** **phone** and **keys**—**pockets** **bulge**, **drop** **items**, and **feel** **unbalanced**. A **light** **backpack** or **crossbody** **keeps** **hands** **free** and **stops** me **losing** **small** **things** in **crowds**.
