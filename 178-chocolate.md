#### 1. Do you like chocolate?
**Yes**, **definitely**—**dark** **chocolate** **especially**, though I **enjoy** **milk** **chocolate** **too** when it's **not** **too** **sweet**. It's **comforting** after a **long** **day** and **easy** to **share** with **friends** over **tea** or **coffee**.

---
#### 2. How often do you eat chocolate?
**A** **few** **times** a **week**, **usually** in **small** **amounts** rather than **whole** **bars** at **once**. During **exam** **periods** or **stressful** **weeks**, I **eat** it **slightly** **more** **often** than I'd **like** to **admit**.

---
#### 3. What kind of chocolate do you like?
**Dark** **chocolate** with **around** **70%** **cocoa**, **plain** **bars** or **simple** **truffles**—**not** **heavily** **filled** **ones** with **artificial** **flavours**. **Occasionally** I **try** **local** **brands** or **gift** **boxes**, but **simplicity** **wins** for **daily** **snacking**.

---
#### 4. Did you often eat chocolate when you were a child?
**Yes**, **especially** on **birthdays**, **holidays**, and **after** **good** **grades**—**parents** **treated** it as a **special** **reward**, **not** an **everyday** **snack**. I **remember** **sharing** **boxes** with **cousins** and **fighting** **politely** over the **last** **piece**.

---
#### 5. Do you give chocolate as a gift?
**Sometimes**—**nicely** **packaged** **boxes** for **birthdays**, **visits**, or **thank**-**you** **gestures** when I **don't** **know** someone's **taste** in **clothes** or **books**. It's **safe**, **affordable**, and **almost** **everyone** **appreciates** it **at** **least** a **little**.

---
#### 6. Is chocolate popular in your country?
**Very**—**international** **brands**, **local** **confectionery**, and **seasonal** **specials** around **festivals** and **Valentine's** **Day** **sell** **heavily**. **Cafés** **also** **use** **chocolate** in **drinks** and **desserts**, so it's **part** of **social** **life**, **not** **just** **home** **snacking**.
