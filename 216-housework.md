#### 1. Do you do housework at home?
**Yes**—I **usually** **wash** the **dishes**, **vacuum**, and **tidy** my **room**. I **don't** **love** it, but **sharing** the **chores** keeps the **place** **liveable**.

---
#### 2. What housework do you dislike the most?
**Cleaning** the **bathroom**—it **takes** **time** and **never** **feels** **fully** **done**. **Ironing** is a **close** **second** because it is **slow** and **easy** to **put** **off**.

---
#### 3. Did you help with housework when you were a child?
**A** **little**—**setting** the **table**, **taking** out the **rubbish**, and **folding** **clothes**. My **parents** **wanted** us to **learn** **responsibility**, not just **wait** to be **served**.

---
#### 4. Do you think children should do housework?
**Yes**—**age**-**appropriate** **tasks** **teach** **discipline** and **teamwork** at **home**. It also **stops** **housework** from **falling** on **one** **person** **all** the **time**.

---
#### 5. How often do you do housework?
**Most** **days** I do **small** **jobs**, and **longer** **cleaning** on the **weekend**. If I'm **busy** with **work** or **study**, the **big** **tidy**-**up** **slips** to **Sunday**.

---
#### 6. Would you hire someone to do housework?
**Possibly** for **deep** **cleaning** if I could **afford** it, but for **daily** **chores** I **prefer** to **manage** them **myself**. **Paying** for **help** is **useful**, not a **replacement** for **basic** **habits**.
