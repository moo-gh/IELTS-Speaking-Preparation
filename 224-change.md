#### 1. Do you like change?
**Mixed** **feelings**—**small** **changes** like **new** **routines** or **meeting** **people** can **feel** **exciting**, but **big** **upheavals** **stress** me **until** I **adapt**. **Overall**, I **see** **change** as **necessary**, even when it's **uncomfortable**.

---
#### 2. What is the biggest change you have experienced recently?
**Probably** **shifting** **more** of my **study** and **social** **life** **online**—**different** **hours**, **fewer** **face**-**to**-**face** **classes**, and **more** **self**-**discipline** at **home**. It **taught** me to **plan** **better** and **not** **rely** on **last**-**minute** **cramming**.

---
#### 3. Are there things in your life you would like to change?
**Yes**—I'd like **more** **consistent** **sleep**, **less** **phone** **scrolling**, and **clearer** **goals** for **English** **practice**. **Changing** **habits** **slowly** **works** **better** for me than **sudden**, **extreme** **rules**.

---
#### 4. Do you think people resist change?
**Often**, **yes**—**familiar** **routines** **feel** **safer**, and **change** can **mean** **risk** or **extra** **effort**. **Support**, **clear** **reasons**, and **time** to **adjust** **help** **people** **accept** it **more** **easily**.

---
#### 5. Is it easier for young people or older people to accept change?
**Young** **people** **often** **adapt** **faster** to **technology** and **new** **ideas**, while **older** **people** may **prefer** **stability** they **trust**. That's **not** **always** **true**—**personality** and **experience** **matter** as **much** as **age**.

---
#### 6. Do you think change is always a good thing?
**Not** **always**—**progress** in **medicine**, **education**, and **rights** can be **wonderful**, but **reckless** **change** without **planning** **hurts** **communities** and **environments**. **The** **key** is **whether** **people** **benefit** **long** **term**, not just **in** the **short** **term**.
