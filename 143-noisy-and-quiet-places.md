#### 1. Do you like quiet or noisy places?
**Quiet** for **focus** and **rest**—**libraries**, **early** **parks**, and **calm** **cafés** **help** me **think**. **Noisy** **places** **work** for **social** **nights** or **markets**, but I **can't** **live** in that **volume** **all** **day**.

---
#### 2. What are some sounds you enjoy?
**Rain** on **windows**, **soft** **café** **chatter**, **birds** in the **morning**, and **instrumental** **music** while I **study**. **Harsh** **drilling**, **constant** **honking**, and **loud** **phone** **speakers** on **buses** **annoy** me **quickly**.

---
#### 3. What kinds of noise do you hear in your daily life?
**Traffic**, **neighbours**, **kitchen** **sounds**, **notifications**, and **occasional** **construction** **near** **campus**. In the **city** **centre** you **add** **sirens**, **vendors**, and the **general** **rush**-**hour** **hum** that **never** **fully** **stops**.

---
#### 4. Is noise louder in cities or in the countryside?
**Cities**, **easily**—**density**, **vehicles**, and **nightlife** **stack** **noise** **layers** **on** **top** of each **other**. The **countryside** **isn't** **silent**, but **sounds** **spread** **out** and **feel** **gentler**, **especially** at **night**.

---
#### 5. Would you choose a quiet or noisy place to spend your free time?
**Usually** **quiet**—after a **busy** **week** I **want** **space** to **read**, **walk**, or **talk** without **shouting**. **Weekends** with **friends** I **accept** **livelier** **spots**, then **recover** somewhere **calmer** **afterwards**.

---
#### 6. Is it easy to find quiet places in your city?
**Not** in the **centre**—you **hunt** for **side** **streets**, **smaller** **parks**, or **library** **corners**. **Early** **mornings** and **late** **evenings** **help**; at **peak** **hours** **true** **quiet** **feels** **rare**.
