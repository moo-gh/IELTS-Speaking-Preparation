#### 1. Which season do you like best?
**Spring** or **early** **autumn**—**mild** **temperatures**, **clearer** **air**, and **enough** **daylight** to **walk** **without** **suffering**. **Peak** **summer** **heat** and **mid**-**winter** **cold** **both** **feel** **harder** to **manage** **daily**.

---
#### 2. What is the weather like in each season where you live?
**Hot**, **dry** **summers**; **cooler** **autumn** **evenings**; **rainy** **winters** with **some** **frost** in **colder** **years**; and **spring** **mixing** **sun**, **wind**, and **sudden** **showers**. **Weather** **swings** **fast**, so **layers** **help** **year**-**round**.

---
#### 3. Do you do different activities in different seasons?
**Yes**—**outdoor** **walks** and **parks** in **spring** and **autumn**, **indoor** **study** and **films** when **heat** or **rain** **hits** **hard**. **Summer** **means** **lighter** **clothes** and **later** **evenings**; **winter** **means** **hot** **drinks** and **earlier** **nights**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer hot or cold weather?
**Mild** **over** **extremes**, but if I **must** **choose**, **slightly** **cool** **weather** **wins**—I **focus** **better** and **sleep** **easier**. **Heavy** **heat** **drains** my **energy** **by** **afternoon**, **especially** in **crowded** **cities**.

---
#### 5. How do seasons affect people's mood in your country?
**Long** **grey** **periods** can **lower** **energy**, while **sunny** **weekends** **fill** **cafés** and **parks** **instantly**. **Holiday** **seasons** **lift** **spirits** **too**, even when **weather** **isn't** **perfect**—**routine** **breaks** **matter** **as** **much** as **temperature**.

---
#### 6. Has the climate changed in your country in recent years?
**Many** **people** **say** **yes**—**hotter** **summers**, **less** **predictable** **rain**, and **more** **extreme** **days** than **a** **decade** **ago**. **Cities** **feel** **warmer** with **concrete** and **traffic**, so **seasons** **don't** **feel** **exactly** like **childhood** **memories**.
