#### 1. Do you like staying with friends?
**Yes**, **when** the **plan** is **relaxed**—**late** **talks**, **shared** **meals**, and **no** **pressure** to **host** **formally**. **Long** **stays** **without** **clear** **boundaries** can **feel** **awkward**, but **short** **visits** **usually** **feel** **fun**.

---
#### 2. How often do you stay at a friend's house?
**Not** **very** **often**—**maybe** a **few** **times** a **year** for **weekend** **trips**, **study** **sessions** that **run** **late**, or **special** **occasions**. **Most** **weeks** I **sleep** at **home** and **meet** **friends** **outside** instead.

---
#### 3. Do you prefer staying with friends or staying at home?
**Home** for **daily** **life**—**my** **routine**, **quiet**, and **own** **space** **matter** for **study**. **Staying** with **friends** **works** **better** as a **short** **break** when I **want** **company** and **a** **change** of **scene**.

---
#### 4. What do you usually do when you stay with friends?
**Cook** **simple** **food**, **watch** **films**, **walk** **nearby** **areas**, **chat** until **late**, and **sometimes** **play** **games** or **study** **together** the **next** **morning**. We **keep** it **low**-**cost** and **low**-**stress** **most** of the **time**.

---
#### 5. Have you ever stayed with a friend for a long time?
**Once**, for **several** **days** during a **trip** when **hotels** **felt** **too** **expensive**. It **worked** because we **planned** **sleep** **times**, **shared** **costs**, and **gave** each **other** **space** when **needed**.

---
#### 6. Is it common for young people to stay with friends in your country?
**Fairly** **common** for **short** **visits**, **trips**, or **events** in **another** **city**, **especially** among **students**. **Long**-**term** **stays** **depend** on **family** **rules**, **housing** **costs**, and **how** **close** the **friendship** **really** is.
