#### 1. Do you often use an umbrella?
**When** it **rains**, **yes**—I **keep** one in my **bag** or by the **door**. On **light** **drizzle** I **sometimes** **skip** it and **wear** a **hood**, but in **heavy** **rain** an **umbrella** is **essential**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever lost an umbrella?
**Several** **times**—**left** on a **bus**, in a **classroom**, or at a **café**. **Cheap** **ones** are **easy** to **replace**, so I **don't** get too **upset**, but **losing** a **sturdy** **pair** is **annoying**.

---
#### 3. Do you prefer umbrellas or raincoats?
**Umbrellas** for **short** **trips** and **city** **walking**, because they **keep** my **bag** and **clothes** **drier**. **Raincoats** are **better** when it's **windy**, since **umbrellas** can **flip** **inside** **out**.

---
#### 4. What colour umbrella do you like?
**Dark** **colours** like **black** or **navy**—they're **practical** and **don't** **show** **dirt** as **quickly**. **Bright** **umbrellas** are **easier** to **spot** in a **crowd**, which is **useful** if I **tend** to **misplace** things.

---
#### 5. Are umbrellas common in your country?
**Very**—**especially** in **rainy** **seasons** and in **cities** where **people** **walk** a **lot**. **Shops** **near** **stations** **sell** them **cheaply** on **wet** **days**, so **almost** **everyone** has **used** one.

---
#### 6. Do you think umbrellas will still be popular in the future?
**Yes**—**weather** **won't** **stop** being **unpredictable**, and **umbrellas** are **simple**, **portable**, and **cheap**. **Better** **designs** may **appear**, but the **basic** **idea** will **stay** **useful**.
