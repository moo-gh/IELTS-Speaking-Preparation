#### 1. Do you like going to concerts?
**Yes**, **when** the **venue** is **comfortable** and the **crowd** **isn't** **too** **crushed**—**live** **energy** **beats** **headphones** for **certain** **songs**. **Huge** **stadium** **shows** can **feel** **overwhelming**, but **smaller** **gigs** **usually** **feel** **worth** the **ticket** **price**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever been to a live music event?
**A** **few** **times**—**local** **bands**, **university** **events**, and **one** **outdoor** **festival** with **friends**. I **remember** the **atmosphere** **more** than **perfect** **sound**, **especially** when **everyone** **sang** **along** **together**.

---
#### 3. What kind of music do you like to listen to live?
**Indie**, **acoustic**, and **some** **pop** **acts** with **strong** **live** **performances**—**music** that **changes** **mood** when you **hear** it **in** a **room** **full** of **people**. **Heavy** **electronic** **sets** **work** **better** **recorded** for me **personally**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer live music or recorded music?
**Recorded** for **daily** **life**—**cheaper**, **easier**, and **repeatable** while I **study** or **commute**. **Live** **music** **wins** for **special** **nights** when I **want** **memory**, **excitement**, and a **break** from **screens**.

---
#### 5. Who do you usually go to concerts with?
**Friends** **mostly**—**shared** **plans**, **split** **costs**, and **someone** to **talk** to during **breaks**. I **wouldn't** **mind** **going** **alone** for an **artist** I **really** **love**, but **group** **trips** **feel** **more** **fun**.

---
#### 6. Are live music events popular in your country?
**Yes**, **especially** in **big** **cities**—**concerts**, **festivals**, and **cultural** **events** **draw** **large** **crowds** on **weekends**. **Ticket** **prices** and **traffic** **limit** **some** **people**, but **interest** in **live** **shows** **remains** **strong**.
