**Very**—**trust** **builds** **slowly** and **collapses** **fast** when **people** **flake** **repeatedly**. **Broken** **promises** **feel** **small** **individually**, but they **poison** **friendships** and **team** **projects** **over** **time**.

**Overcommitment**, **forgetfulness**, **sudden** **emergencies**, or **fear** of **saying** **no** **upfront**. **Sometimes** **people** **mean** **well** but **underestimate** **workload**; **other** **times** they **never** **intended** to **follow** **through**.

---

**Yes**—**friends** **cancelled** **trips** **last** **minute**, and **once** a **group** **mate** **vanished** on **shared** **work** **deadlines**. **Honest** **explanations** **heal** things **faster** than **silence** or **excuses** **that** **shift** **blame**.

---
#### 5. Do you think reliability is important?
**Essential**—**employers**, **classmates**, and **family** **all** **depend** on **knowing** you'll **show** **up** or **warn** **early** when you **can't**. **Reliability** **beats** **charisma** **long** **term**, **in** my **experience**.

---
#### 6. Why are some people unreliable?
**Poor** **planning**, **chronic** **lateness**, **avoidance** of **conflict**, or **treating** **commitments** **casually** because **others** **always** **cover** for them. **Stress** and **mental** **health** **struggles** **play** a **role** **too**—**not** **always** **selfishness** **alone**.
