#### 1. Do you like going to the theatre?
**Yes**, **when** the **story** is **strong** and the **seats** are **comfortable** enough to **focus** for **two** **hours**. **Live** **performance** **feels** **more** **immediate** than **films**, though **ticket** **prices** and **travel** **time** **limit** how **often** I **go**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever been to the theatre?
**A** **few** **times**—**school** **trips**, **university** **events**, and **one** **local** **play** with **friends** **during** a **holiday**. I **remember** the **lighting** and **audience** **reactions** **more** than **every** **line** of **dialogue**.

---
#### 3. What kind of plays or performances do you like?
**Drama** with **clear** **characters** and **some** **humour**, **not** **overly** **abstract** **experimental** **pieces** that **leave** me **confused**. **Musicals** are **fun** **occasionally**, but I **prefer** **stories** where **acting** **carries** most of the **weight**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer theatre or cinema?
**Cinema** for **convenience** and **variety**—**cheaper**, **easier** to **schedule**, and **better** for **quick** **evening** **plans**. **Theatre** **wins** when I **want** a **special** **night** out and the **energy** of a **live** **room** **matters**.

---
#### 5. Is theatre popular in your country?
**In** **big** **cities**, **yes**—**national** **theatres**, **festivals**, and **touring** **shows** **attract** **students**, **tourists**, and **older** **audiences**. **Smaller** **towns** **see** **less** **theatre** **unless** **travelling** **companies** **visit** **during** **holiday** **seasons**.

---
#### 6. Would you like to act in a play?
**Maybe** a **small** **role** for **fun**, but **not** as a **career**—**memorising** **lines** and **performing** in **front** of **strangers** **sounds** **nerve**-**wracking**. I'd **rather** **support** from the **audience** unless a **friend** **dragged** me into a **light** **comedy** **skit**.
