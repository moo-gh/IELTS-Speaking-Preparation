#### 1. Are you an introvert or an extrovert?
**Somewhere** in the **middle**—I **enjoy** **social** **time**, but I **need** **quiet** **hours** to **recover** **after** **long** **events**. **Labels** **help** **slightly**, yet **context** **matters** **more** than **fixed** **categories** for me.

---
#### 2. What kind of personality do you have?
**Calm**, **curious**, **slightly** **perfectionist**, and **sometimes** **too** **self**-**critical** when **results** **disappoint** me. **Friends** **say** I'm **reliable** and **a** **good** **listener**, though I **can** **overthink** **small** **decisions** **before** **acting**.

---
#### 3. Do you think personality can change?
**Yes**, **gradually**—**experience**, **travel**, **failure**, and **close** **relationships** **shift** **habits** **over** **years**. **Core** **traits** **stay** **partly** **stable**, but **maturity** **teaches** me to **manage** **traits** that **used** to **cause** **trouble**.

---
#### 4. Is personality important in friendships?
**Very**—**shared** **values**, **humour**, and **conflict** **style** **matter** **more** than **surface** **similarity**. **Opposite** **personalities** can **work** when **both** **people** **respect** **differences** **instead** of **trying** to **fix** each **other**.

---
#### 5. Are you similar to your family members in personality?
**Partly**—I **inherited** some **stubbornness** and **sense** of **duty**, but I'm **less** **outspoken** than **certain** **relatives**. **Sibling** **differences** **showed** me **early** that **same** **home** **doesn't** **mean** **same** **temperament**.

---
#### 6. What personality traits are valued in your country?
**Honesty**, **hard** **work**, **respect** for **elders**, **hospitality**, and **emotional** **control** in **public** **settings** **often** **get** **praised**. **Creativity** and **assertiveness** **gain** **respect** **too**, **especially** in **urban** **professional** **life**.
