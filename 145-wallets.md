#### 1. Do you use a wallet?
**Yes**—mainly for **cards**, **ID**, and a **little** **cash** when **shops** **won't** **take** **phones**. Mine is a **slim** **card** **holder** **rather** than a **bulky** **leather** **wallet** **stuffed** with **old** **receipts**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever lost your wallet?
**Once**—I **left** it on a **café** **counter** and **panicked** until the **staff** **called** me **back**. Since then I **pat** my **pocket** or **bag** before **leaving** **anywhere**, **especially** after **paying**.

---
#### 3. Would you buy a wallet as a gift for someone?
**Sometimes**—for **people** who **still** **carry** **cards** and **cash**, a **good** **wallet** **feels** **practical** and **lasting**. I'd **check** their **style** **first**; a **wrong** **colour** or **size** **ends** up **unused** in a **drawer**.

---
#### 4. Do most of your friends use a wallet?
**Most** **do**, even if they **pay** by **phone** **daily**—they **still** **need** **ID**, **bank** **cards**, and **backup** **cash**. A **few** **rely** almost **entirely** on **mobile** **payments** and **keep** **cards** in a **phone** **case**.

---
#### 5. What do you usually keep in your wallet?
**Debit** **card**, **student**/**ID** **card**, **one** or **two** **loyalty** **cards**, and **small** **notes** for **taxis** or **markets**. I **try** not to **overstuff** it—**extra** **paper** **makes** it **hard** to **close** and **easy** to **lose** **track** of.

---
#### 6. Do you think people will stop using wallets in the future?
**Maybe** **less**, not **completely**—**phones** and **digital** **IDs** **cover** **many** **payments**, but **physical** **cards** and **backup** **cash** **still** **matter** when **batteries** **die** or **systems** **fail**. **Slim** **card** **holders** will **probably** **outlast** **traditional** **fat** **wallets**.
