#### 1. Do you like going to markets?
**Yes**, **especially** **outdoor** **markets** with **fresh** **produce**, **street** **food**, and **lively** **noise**—they **feel** **more** **human** than **silent** **supermarket** **aisles**. **Crowds** can **tire** me **on** **hot** **days**, but the **colours** and **smells** **usually** **make** it **worth** it.

---
#### 2. What can you buy at markets in your country?
**Fruit**, **vegetables**, **herbs**, **spices**, **bread**, **cheese**, **clothes**, **household** **goods**, and **occasionally** **handmade** **crafts** or **flowers**. **Some** **markets** **specialise** in **antiques** or **second**-**hand** **books**, **depending** on the **city** and **day** of the **week**.

---
#### 3. How often do you go to markets?
**Maybe** **once** a **month** for **fresh** **ingredients** or **weekend** **wandering** with **friends**. **Daily** **shopping** **happens** **more** at **supermarkets** **near** **home**, but **markets** **draw** me when I **want** **variety** or **better** **seasonal** **prices**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer markets or supermarkets?
**Markets** for **freshness** and **atmosphere**; **supermarkets** for **convenience**, **fixed** **prices**, and **one**-**stop** **buying** **late** at **night**. **Ideally** I **combine** **both**—**markets** for **produce**, **supermarkets** for **packaged** **basics**.

---
#### 5. Are markets popular in your country?
**Very**, **especially** in **older** **city** **centres** and **town** **squares** where **tradition** **still** **runs** **strong**. **Tourists** **love** them **too**, which **sometimes** **pushes** **prices** **up**, but **locals** **still** **rely** on them for **affordable** **daily** **goods**.

---
#### 6. What is the atmosphere like at markets?
**Busy**, **noisy**, and **slightly** **chaotic**—**vendors** **calling** **prices**, **bargaining** **here** and **there**, **music** **sometimes**, and **constant** **movement**. **Early** **morning** **feels** **calmer**; **late** **morning** **weekends** **feel** like a **festival** **without** a **stage**.
