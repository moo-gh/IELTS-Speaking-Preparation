#### 1. Do you follow a special diet?
**Not** **strictly**—I **try** to **eat** **balanced** **meals** with **enough** **vegetables**, **protein**, and **water**, but I **don't** **label** myself **vegan** or **keto**. **Exam** **weeks** **push** me toward **quick** **carbs** and **coffee**, which **isn't** **ideal**, **honestly**.

---
#### 2. What is a healthy diet in your opinion?
**Variety**, **moderate** **portions**, **less** **processed** **sugar**, **regular** **meals**, and **enough** **fiber** from **fruit** and **whole** **grains**. **Healthy** **doesn't** **mean** **boring**—**home** **cooking** with **spices** **beats** **plain** **salads** you **quit** after **three** **days**.

---
#### 3. Have your eating habits changed over time?
**Yes**—**childhood** **meals** were **more** **family**-**controlled**; **now** I **choose** **speed** **over** **nutrition** **sometimes**, then **correct** with **simpler** **food** when I **feel** **sluggish**. **Studying** **abroad** or **living** **alone** **taught** me to **plan** **groceries** **instead** of **ordering** **daily**.

---
#### 4. Do people in your country care about diet?
**More** than **before**—**fitness** **influencers**, **diabetes** **awareness**, and **weight** **concerns** **appear** in **media** **often**. **Still**, **traditional** **rich** **dishes** **remain** **central** at **celebrations**, so **balance** **between** **health** **goals** and **culture** **can** **feel** **tricky**.

---
#### 5. Is it easy to eat healthily where you live?
**Moderately**—**supermarkets** **stock** **fresh** **produce**, but **cheap** **fast** **food** and **late**-**night** **delivery** **test** **willpower** **daily**. **Cooking** at **home** **helps**; **eating** **out** **often** **makes** **salt** and **oil** **hard** to **control**.

---
#### 6. Do you read food labels when you shop?
**Sometimes**, **especially** for **snacks**, **yogurt**, and **anything** **marked** **"low** **fat"** that **hides** **extra** **sugar**. I **don't** **obsess** over **every** **gram**, but **checking** **ingredients** **occasionally** **stops** me **buying** **marketing** **tricks** **blindly**.
