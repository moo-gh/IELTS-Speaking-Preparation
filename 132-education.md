#### 1. Did you enjoy school as a child?
**Mostly**, **yes**—**friends**, **sports**, and **story** **books** **made** **weeks** **bearable**, even when **homework** **felt** **endless**. **Strict** **exams** **stressed** me **later**, but **primary** **school** **felt** **lighter** and **more** **playful**.

---
#### 2. What was your favourite subject?
**English** and **history**—**stories**, **debates**, and **learning** **how** **past** **events** **shape** **today** **interested** me **more** than **memorising** **formulas** **alone**. **Literature** **classes** **also** **helped** my **writing** **confidence** **gradually**.

---
#### 3. Was there any subject you did not like?
**Advanced** **maths** at **times**—**abstract** **topics** **without** **clear** **examples** **frustrated** me until a **patient** **teacher** **showed** **practical** **uses**. **Rote** **learning** **subjects** **bored** me **fast** if **discussion** **wasn't** **allowed**.

---
#### 4. Did you have a favourite teacher at school?
**Yes**, an **English** **teacher** who **encouraged** **questions** and **gave** **honest** **feedback** **without** **embarrassing** **students**. **History** **teachers** who **told** **events** like **dramas** **also** **stuck** in my **memory**.

---
#### 5. Did you ever do any extra-curricular activities?
**Football**, **debate** **club** **briefly**, and **school** **magazine** **writing** when **schedules** **allowed**. **Music** **lessons** **lasted** **one** **term** before I **admitted** I **preferred** **running** **outside**.

---
#### 6. Are you currently learning anything new?
**Yes**—**English** **for** **IELTS**, **better** **academic** **writing**, and **occasional** **online** **courses** in **subjects** **linked** to my **degree**. **Learning** **never** **really** **stops** once you **leave** **formal** **school**, **honestly**.
