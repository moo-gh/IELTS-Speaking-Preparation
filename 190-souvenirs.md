#### 1. Do you like buying souvenirs when you travel?
**Yes**, but **in** **small** **amounts**—**one** or **two** **items** that **remind** me of the **place** **without** **filling** my **bag**. I **avoid** **mass**-**produced** **junk** that **looks** **identical** in **every** **airport** **shop**.

---
#### 2. What kind of souvenirs do people buy in your country?
**Local** **crafts**, **spices**, **tea**, **postcards**, **magnets**, **textiles**, and **sometimes** **food** **specialities** that **travel** **well**. **Tourist** **areas** **sell** **mix** of **authentic** **handmade** **goods** and **cheap** **factory** **copies**.

---
#### 3. What was the last souvenir you bought?
**A** **small** **notebook** with a **local** **landmark** on the **cover** from a **short** **trip** **last** **year**—**light**, **useful**, and **not** **embarrassingly** **tacky**. I **also** **kept** **ticket** **stubs** and **photos**, which **count** as **free** **souvenirs** for me.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer useful souvenirs or decorative ones?
**Useful** **slightly**—**mugs**, **notebooks**, or **kitchen** **items** I **actually** **use** at **home**. **Decorative** **pieces** are **fine** if they're **small** and **meaningful**, but **shelves** **fill** up **fast** if I **buy** **too** **many** **ornaments**.

---
#### 5. Do you give souvenirs as gifts?
**Sometimes**—**snacks**, **tea**, or **small** **craft** **items** for **family** when **returning** from **trips**. **Gifts** **work** **best** when they **match** the **person's** **taste**, **not** when I **dump** **random** **tourist** **stock** on **everyone**.

---
#### 6. Are souvenirs important when travelling?
**To** **some** **extent**—they **anchor** **memories** and **support** **local** **makers** when **chosen** **carefully**. **Experiences** **matter** **more** than **objects**, but a **modest** **souvenir** can **trigger** **stories** **years** **later** when you **see** it on a **desk** or **shelf**.
