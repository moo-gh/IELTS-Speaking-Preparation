#### 1. Have you ever travelled by plane?
**Yes**, **several** **times**—**domestic** **trips** for **holidays** and **one** or **two** **international** **flights** for **study**-**related** **travel**. **Airports** **stress** me **slightly** because of **security** **checks** and **tight** **schedules**, but **flying** **still** **feels** **exciting**.

---
#### 2. Do you like flying?
**Mostly** **yes**—**takeoff** and **views** from the **window** are **amazing**, and **long** **distances** **shrink** **dramatically**. **Turbulence**, **cramped** **seats**, and **jet** **lag** **make** **some** **flights** **uncomfortable**, **especially** in **economy** **class**.

---
#### 3. What do you usually do on a plane?
**Listen** to **music**, **watch** a **film**, **read**, or **try** to **sleep** if the **timing** **allows**. On **shorter** **flights** I **mostly** **stare** out the **window** or **plan** what I'll **do** **after** **landing**.

---
#### 4. Is flying popular in your country?
**Very**—**major** **cities** have **busy** **airports**, and **many** **families** **fly** for **holidays**, **work**, or **visiting** **relatives** **abroad**. **Budget** **airlines** **made** **domestic** **travel** **more** **common** than **a** **generation** **ago**.

---
#### 5. Would you like to be a pilot?
**Not** **really**—the **responsibility** and **training** **sound** **intense**, and **irregular** **hours** would **disrupt** **sleep** and **family** **life**. I **admire** **pilots**, but I **prefer** **being** a **passenger** with **less** **pressure**.

---
#### 6. What's the longest flight you've taken?
**Around** **eight** to **ten** **hours**, **including** **one** **stop** **overseas** for **study**-**related** **travel**. **Long** **flights** **test** my **patience**, so I **pack** **snacks**, **headphones**, and **offline** **entertainment** to **survive** **boredom**.
