#### 1. Do you like travelling?
**Yes**, **mostly**—**new** **places**, **food**, and **breaks** from **routine** **recharge** me **fast**. **Long** **journeys** with **tight** **schedules** **feel** **exhausting**, but **well**-**planned** **trips** **usually** **leave** **good** **memories**.

---
#### 2. Where did you go on your last holiday?
**A** **nearby** **city** for a **long** **weekend**—**museums**, **walking** **old** **streets**, and **trying** **local** **cafés** **without** **rushing** **every** **hour**. It **wasn't** **abroad**, but it **felt** like a **proper** **escape** from **study** **stress**.

---
#### 3. What kind of holidays do you prefer?
**Relaxed** **city** **breaks** or **nature** **trips** with **light** **plans**—**enough** **structure** to **see** **highlights**, **enough** **free** **time** to **wander**. **Packed** **tours** with **non**-**stop** **sightseeing** **burn** me **out** **quickly**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer travelling alone or with others?
**With** **friends** or **family** for **most** **trips**—**shared** **meals** and **inside** **jokes** **make** **travel** **richer**. **Solo** **travel** **appeals** for **short** **personal** **breaks** when I **want** **quiet** and **flexibility**.

---
#### 5. What do you usually do when you travel?
**Walk** **a** **lot**, **try** **local** **food**, **visit** **one** or **two** **main** **sights** **per** **day**, and **leave** **space** for **unexpected** **discoveries**. I **also** **take** **photos**, but I **try** not to **experience** **everything** **through** a **screen**.

---
#### 6. Would you like to travel more in the future?
**Definitely**—**budget** and **time** **limit** me **now**, but I **hope** to **visit** **more** **countries**, **improve** my **English** **on** the **road**, and **learn** how **different** **cities** **handle** **daily** **life**.
