#### 1. Do you like baking?
**Yes**, **though** I'm **not** **expert**—**simple** **recipes** like **cookies**, **muffins**, or **flatbread** **feel** **relaxing** when the **kitchen** **smells** **warm** and **sweet**. **Complex** **pastries** **stress** me because **timing** and **temperature** **must** be **exact**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever baked anything?
**Many** **times**—**birthday** **cakes** with **family**, **exam**-**week** **banana** **bread**, and **occasional** **pizza** **dough** when **takeaway** **felt** **too** **expensive**. **Failures** **happened** **too**—**dense** **cakes** and **burnt** **edges** **taught** me to **read** **recipes** **twice**.

---
#### 3. What do people usually bake in your country?
**Bread**, **pastries**, **cookies**, **traditional** **cakes** for **holidays**, and **savoury** **pies** in **some** **regions**. **Home** **ovens** **work** **harder** during **festivals**, when **families** **prepare** **desserts** for **guests** and **neighbours**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer baking or cooking?
**Cooking** for **daily** **meals**—**faster**, **more** **forgiving**, and **easier** to **improvise**. **Baking** **wins** for **weekends** and **celebrations** when I **want** something **special** that **fills** the **flat** with **good** **smells**.

---
#### 5. Would you like to learn more about baking?
**Yes**—**basic** **bread**, **better** **decoration**, and **healthier** **substitutes** for **sugar** and **butter** would **help** me **host** **friends** **without** **buying** **everything** from **shops**. **Short** **classes** or **video** **tutorials** would **suit** my **schedule** **best**.

---
#### 6. Is baking popular in your country?
**Very**, **especially** at **home** and in **small** **bakeries** that **compete** with **supermarket** **bread**. **Social** **media** **boosted** **interest** in **fancy** **desserts**, but **traditional** **recipes** **still** **matter** for **family** **gatherings** and **religious** **holidays**.
