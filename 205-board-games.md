#### 1. Do you like playing board games?
**Yes**, **especially** **strategy** and **word** **games** that **keep** **everyone** **talking** **without** **staring** at **separate** **screens**. **Overly** **long** **rulebooks** **tire** me, but **a** **clear** **game** with **friends** **feels** **worth** the **setup** **time**.

---
#### 2. What board games are popular in your country?
**Chess**, **backgammon**, **card** **games**, **family** **classics** like **Monopoly**-**style** **games**, and **modern** **party** **games** **imported** from **abroad**. **Older** **generations** **play** **traditional** **games** at **gatherings**, while **students** **try** **newer** **titles** from **online** **reviews**.

---
#### 3. Who do you usually play board games with?
**Friends**, **cousins**, and **sometimes** **classmates** during **breaks** or **holiday** **visits**. **Serious** **chess** **happens** **one**-**on**-**one**; **party** **games** **work** **better** with **larger** **groups** and **snacks** on the **table**.

---
#### 4. Did you play board games when you were a child?
**Yes**—**simple** **games** with **parents**, **card** **tricks**, and **school** **clubs** that **introduced** **chess** **basics**. **Rainy** **days** and **power** **cuts** **made** **board** **games** **more** **common** than **today**, when **phones** **fill** the **gaps**.

---
#### 5. Do you prefer board games or video games?
**Board** **games** for **face**-**to**-**face** **fun** and **real** **laughter**; **video** **games** when I **want** **solo** **relaxation** or **online** **friends** **far** **away**. **Ideally** I **mix** **both**, but **in**-**person** **games** **feel** **warmer** for **family** **nights**.

---
#### 6. Is playing board games a good way to spend time with family?
**Excellent**—**shared** **rules**, **light** **competition**, and **no** **need** for **perfect** **skills** **keep** **generations** **connected**. **Even** **short** **rounds** **before** **bed** **beat** **everyone** **scrolling** in **silence** in the **same** **room**.
