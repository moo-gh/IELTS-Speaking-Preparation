#### 1. What do you usually have for breakfast?
**Something** **simple**—**toast**, **eggs**, **yogurt**, **fruit**, or **cereal** with **tea** or **coffee** when I'm **not** **rushed**. On **busy** **mornings** I **grab** **whatever** is **fastest**, which **isn't** **always** the **healthiest** **choice**, **honestly**.

---
#### 2. Do you think breakfast is important?
**Yes**, **especially** for **focus** in the **morning**—**skipping** it **too** **often** **leaves** me **hungry** and **distracted** **before** **lunch**. It's **not** **magic**, but a **decent** **breakfast** **sets** a **steadier** **rhythm** for the **rest** of the **day**.

---
#### 3. Do you ever skip breakfast?
**Sometimes**, when I **wake** up **late** or **feel** **too** **nervous** **before** an **exam** to **eat** **much**. I **try** not to **make** it a **habit**, because **mid**-**morning** **crashes** **hit** **harder** than **saving** **ten** **minutes** at **home**.

---
#### 4. What do people in your country usually eat for breakfast?
**Bread**, **cheese**, **eggs**, **tea**, **local** **dishes** like **soup** or **rice**-**based** **meals** in **some** **regions**, and **more** **Western** **options** in **cities**—**cereal**, **pastries**, and **coffee** **to** **go**. **Weekend** **breakfasts** **tend** to be **longer** and **more** **social** than **weekday** **ones**.

---
#### 5. Do you prefer to eat breakfast at home or outside?
**Home**, **usually**—**cheaper**, **calmer**, and **easier** to **control** what I **eat**. **Cafés** are **nice** on **weekends** or **when** **meeting** **friends**, but **daily** **takeaway** **breakfast** **adds** up **fast** in **cost** and **packaging**.

---
#### 6. Has your breakfast habit changed over time?
**Yes**—as a **child** I **ate** **whatever** **parents** **prepared**; **now** I **choose** **speed** **over** **variety** during **study** **years**. I'm **trying** to **shift** **back** towards **more** **protein** and **fruit** instead of **relying** on **sugar** **snacks** **before** **class**.
