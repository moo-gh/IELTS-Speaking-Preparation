#### 1. Do you like competition?
**In** **moderation**, **yes**—it **pushes** me to **prepare** **harder** and **measure** **progress** **honestly**. **Constant** **competition** **everywhere** **feels** **toxic**, **especially** when **people** **compare** **lives** on **social** **media** **without** **context**.

---
#### 2. Are you a competitive person?
**Somewhat**—I **care** about **personal** **goals** and **doing** **better** than my **past** **self**, but I **don't** **need** to **beat** **everyone** in the **room**. **Team** **settings** **bring** out **healthy** **competition**; **petty** **rivalries** **drain** my **energy** **quickly**.

---
#### 3. What kind of competitions are popular in your country?
**Sports** **leagues**, **school** **exams**, **university** **entrance** **tests**, **singing** and **talent** **shows**, and **business** **pitch** **events** in **cities**. **Online** **gaming** and **e**-**sports** **competitions** **grew** **fast** among **younger** **audiences** **too**.

---
#### 4. Have you ever taken part in a competition?
**Yes**—**debates**, **science** **fairs**, **sports** **matches**, and **language** **contests** at **school** and **university**. I **remember** **nerves** **before** **performing**, but **preparation** **usually** **reduced** the **fear** **enough** to **focus**.

---
#### 5. Do you think competition is good for children?
**Balanced** **competition** can **build** **resilience**, **discipline**, and **teamwork**, but **pressure** to **win** **at** **all** **costs** **hurts** **confidence**. **Children** **need** **support** when they **lose**, **not** **shame**, or they **start** **avoiding** **challenges** **altogether**.

---
#### 6. Is there too much competition in modern life?
**Often**, **yes**—**jobs**, **grades**, **appearance**, and **online** **metrics** **turn** **daily** **life** into a **scoreboard**. **Some** **competition** **drives** **innovation**, but **rest**, **cooperation**, and **non**-**competitive** **hobbies** **balance** it **badly** **needed**.
