#### 1. What motivates you in daily life?
**Clear** **goals**, **supportive** **people**, **small** **wins**, and **fear** of **wasting** **opportunities** I **worked** **hard** to **earn**. **Visible** **progress**—**better** **grades**, **stronger** **English**, **healthier** **habits**—**keeps** me **moving** when **motivation** **drops**.

---
#### 2. Are you a motivated person?
**Mostly**, but **in** **waves**—I **start** **strong** after **planning**, then **dip** when **tasks** **feel** **endless** or **lonely**. **Deadlines** **rescue** me **often**, though I'd **prefer** **steady** **drive** **without** **last**-**minute** **panic**.

---
#### 3. What demotivates you?
**Vague** **instructions**, **constant** **criticism** **without** **help**, **comparison** on **social** **media**, and **burnout** from **saying** **yes** to **too** **many** **commitments**. **Bad** **sleep** and **hunger** **demotivate** me **faster** than **hard** **work** **itself**.

---
#### 4. How do you stay motivated when studying?
**Break** **material** into **chunks**, **reward** **myself** **after** **sessions**, **study** with **friends**, and **remind** myself **why** the **exam** **matters** for **my** **future**. **Changing** **location**—**library**, **café**, **quiet** **desk**—**stops** **boredom** from **killing** **focus**.

---
#### 5. Is motivation important for success?
**Yes**, but **not** **alone**—**discipline**, **strategy**, and **support** **matter** **equally**. **Pure** **enthusiasm** **fades**; **systems** and **habits** **carry** you when **motivation** **disappears** for **days** or **weeks**.

---
#### 6. What motivates young people in your country?
**Careers**, **travel**, **financial** **independence**, **family** **pride**, and **online** **role** **models** **mix** **together**, **sometimes** **conflicting**. **Competition** **motivates** **some** **students** **heavily**; **others** **burn** **out** and **seek** **meaning** **outside** **rankings** **instead**.
