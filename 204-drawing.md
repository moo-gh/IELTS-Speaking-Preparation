#### 1. Do you like drawing?
**Yes**, **casually**—**doodling** in **notebooks**, **sketching** **objects** on the **desk**, and **simple** **diagrams** for **study** **notes** **relax** me when I'm **stuck** on **text**. I'm **not** a **trained** **artist**, but **drawing** **helps** me **think** **visually**.

---
#### 2. Did you learn to draw at school?
**Basic** **lessons** in **primary** **school**—**shapes**, **perspective** **intro**, and **colour** **mixing** with **cheap** **pencils** and **watercolours**. **Later** **years** **focused** **more** on **exams**, so **formal** **art** **training** **stopped** **unless** you **chose** it **as** an **elective**.

---
#### 3. How often do you draw?
**A** **few** **times** a **month**, **more** when I **need** a **break** from **screens** or **plan** **posters** for **group** **work**. **Daily** **drawing** **isn't** my **habit**, but **quick** **sketches** **appear** in **margins** **weekly**.

---
#### 4. What do you like to draw?
**Simple** **landscapes**, **faces** **roughly**, **buildings**, and **cartoon**-**style** **figures** when I'm **joking** with **friends**. **Still** **life** **bores** me **unless** the **lighting** **looks** **dramatic** **enough** to **motivate** me.

---
#### 5. Would you like to improve your drawing skills?
**Yes**—**better** **proportion**, **shading**, and **digital** **tools** would **help** my **presentations** and **personal** **projects**. **Short** **online** **courses** or **weekend** **workshops** **sound** **realistic** if I **schedule** them **properly**.

---

**Very**—**colouring** **books**, **school** **competitions**, and **tablet** **apps** **keep** it **alive** **despite** **phones**. **Parents** **often** **encourage** it as **creativity** **before** **children** **shift** **fully** to **gaming** and **video** **content**.
