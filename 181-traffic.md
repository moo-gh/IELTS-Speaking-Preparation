#### 1. Is there a lot of traffic in your city?
**Yes**, **especially** during **morning** and **evening** **rush** **hours**—**main** **roads** **fill** **quickly** with **cars**, **buses**, and **delivery** **motorbikes**. **Weekends** are **better**, but **popular** **shopping** **areas** **still** **get** **crowded** **mid**-**day**.

---
#### 2. How do you feel when you are stuck in traffic?
**Frustrated** and **slightly** **helpless**—**watching** the **clock** while **moving** **centimetres** **every** **few** **minutes** **wastes** **energy** I **could** **use** **studying** or **resting**. **Podcasts** or **music** **help**, but they **don't** **fix** the **delay** **itself**.

---
#### 3. How can traffic problems be solved?
**Better** **public** **transport**, **bus** **lanes**, **parking** **rules**, **remote** **work** **options**, and **discouraging** **unnecessary** **private** **trips** during **peak** **times**. **Long**-**term**, **city** **planning** that **mixes** **housing**, **jobs**, and **services** **more** **evenly** would **help** **too**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer to travel by car or public transport?
**Public** **transport** in **cities**—**cheaper**, **less** **stressful** than **driving**, and **better** for the **environment** when **services** **run** **often**. **Cars** **make** **sense** for **family** **trips** or **areas** with **weak** **bus** or **train** **links**.

---
#### 5. Has traffic changed in your city in recent years?
**Yes**—**more** **cars**, **more** **delivery** **vehicles**, and **constant** **road** **works** **seem** to have **increased** **congestion** **overall**. **New** **metro** **lines** **helped** **some** **corridors**, but **growth** **outpaced** **infrastructure** in **many** **districts**.

---
#### 6. What time of day is traffic worst?
**Around** **eight** to **nine** in the **morning** and **five** to **seven** in the **evening**, when **schools** and **offices** **start** or **finish** **at** **similar** **times**. **Friday** **evenings** and **holiday** **eve** **traffic** can be **even** **worse** than **normal** **weekdays**.
