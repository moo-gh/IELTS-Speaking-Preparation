#### 1. Do you often procrastinate?
**Yes**, **more** than I'd **like**—I **delay** **starting** **essays**, **replies**, and **tidy**-**up** **tasks** until **pressure** **forces** me to **move**. **Short** **breaks** **turn** into **long** **scrolls** **easily** when I'm **tired** or **unsure** where to **begin**.

---
#### 2. What do you usually procrastinate on?
**Writing** **assignments**, **admin** **forms**, **laundry**, and **anything** that **needs** **sustained** **focus** **without** **instant** **reward**. **Studying** **vocabulary** **gets** **postponed** **too**, even though I **know** **daily** **practice** **works** **better** than **cramming**.

---
#### 3. Why do people procrastinate?
**Fear** of **failure**, **perfectionism**, **boredom**, **distraction**, and **tasks** that **feel** **too** **big** to **start**. **Phones** and **social** **media** **offer** **easy** **escape**, so **avoidance** **feels** **comfortable** **until** **deadlines** **punish** it.

---
#### 4. How does procrastination affect you?
**Stress** **spikes** **near** **deadlines**, **sleep** **suffers**, **work** **quality** **drops**, and I **snap** at **small** **interruptions** because I'm **already** **guilty**. **Last**-**minute** **rushes** **sometimes** **produce** **acceptable** **results**, but **rarely** my **best** **work**.

---
#### 5. What do you do to stop procrastinating?
**Break** **tasks** into **tiny** **steps**, **set** **timers**, **remove** **phone** **from** the **desk**, and **tell** a **friend** my **plan** so **accountability** **helps**. **Starting** for **five** **minutes** **often** **tricks** my **brain** into **continuing** **longer** than I **expected**.

---
#### 6. Is procrastination common among students in your country?
**Very**—**exam** **culture**, **heavy** **phone** **use**, and **competition** **make** **many** **students** **work** in **panic** **cycles** **instead** of **steady** **rhythms**. **Teachers** **warn** about it **constantly**, but **habits** **change** **slowly** **without** **real** **time**-**management** **training**.
