#### 1. What is your favourite animal?
**Dogs**, **mostly**—**loyal**, **expressive**, and **good** at **reading** **moods** without **needing** **words**. **Cats** **rank** **second** for **independence** and **quiet** **companionship** when you **want** **calm** at **home**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever had a pet before?
**Yes**, we **kept** a **cat** when I was **younger**—**independent**, **soft**, and **occasionally** **demanding** at **meal** **times**. It **taught** me **basic** **responsibility**, like **feeding** **schedules** and **not** **disturbing** her when she **wanted** **sleep**.

---
#### 3. Would you like to have a pet in the future?
**Maybe**, if my **housing** and **schedule** **allow** it—**rental** **rules**, **travel**, and **costs** **matter** **before** **committing**. A **small** **low**-**maintenance** **pet** **appeals** **more** than a **high**-**energy** **dog** **right** **now**.

---
#### 4. What is the most popular pet in your country?
**Cats** and **dogs** **lead**, **especially** in **cities** where **people** **want** **companionship** in **apartments**. **Birds**, **fish**, and **small** **rodents** **appear** **too**, **often** for **families** who **want** **pets** **without** **much** **space**.

---
#### 5. Do you prefer to keep pets indoors or outdoors?
**Indoors**, **generally**—**safer** from **traffic**, **weather**, and **stray** **conflicts**, and **easier** to **monitor** **health**. **Outdoor** **access** **works** for **some** **dogs** with **secure** **yards**, but **city** **life** **usually** **limits** that.

---
#### 6. Do you think pets are good for children?
**Yes**, **with** **adult** **supervision**—**pets** **teach** **care**, **patience**, and **empathy**, and they **give** **comfort** during **stressful** **periods**. **Allergies**, **cost**, and **long**-**term** **commitment** **still** **need** **serious** **family** **discussion** **first**.
