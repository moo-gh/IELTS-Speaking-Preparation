#### 1. How do people show politeness in your country?
**Greetings**, **offering** **tea**, **using** **titles** for **elders**, **waiting** **turns** in **queues**, and **softening** **disagreement** with **phrases** like "with **respect**". **Hospitality** **matters** **deeply**—**guests** **shouldn't** **feel** **rushed** or **ignored**.

---
#### 2. Are children usually encouraged to be more polite to older people?
**Yes**, **strongly**—**parents** **correct** **rude** **tone**, **remind** kids to **greet** **relatives**, and **expect** **help** carrying **bags** or **offering** **seats**. **Schools** **reinforce** the **same** **rules** with **classroom** **manners**.

---
#### 3. Was it your teachers or your parents who taught you to be polite as a child?
**Both**, but **parents** **set** the **daily** **tone** at **home**, while **teachers** **enforced** **public** **behaviour** like **queueing** and **not** **interrupting**. **Grandparents** **added** **extra** **stories** about **respect** during **family** **visits**.

---
#### 4. How do schools encourage politeness?
**Classroom** **rules**, **assembly** **talks**, **peer** **monitor** **roles**, and **teachers** **modelling** **calm** **language** even when **students** **misbehave**. **Praise** for **helpful** **behaviour** **works** **better** than **public** **shaming**, **in** my **experience**.

---
#### 5. Do you think people these days are less polite than in the past?
**Mixed**—**online** **arguments** **feel** **harsher**, and **phones** **distract** people in **queues**, but **many** **still** **show** **warm** **hospitality** **face**-**to**-**face**. **Stress** and **speed** **hurt** **manners** **more** than **morals** **vanishing** **entirely**.

---
#### 6. Do you ever lose your patience?
**Sometimes**, **when** **traffic**, **noise**, or **repeated** **excuses** **stack** **up** after a **long** **day**. I **try** to **pause**, **breathe**, and **respond** **quietly** rather than **escalate**—**regret** **follows** **sharp** **words** **fast**.
