#### 1. Do you drink alcohol?
**Rarely**—**occasional** **sips** at **family** **celebrations** or **toasts**, but **not** **regular** **drinking** during **study** **weeks**. I **prefer** **tea**, **coffee**, or **water** for **daily** **life** because **alcohol** **makes** me **sleepy** and **less** **focused**.

---
#### 2. What kind of alcoholic drinks are popular in your country?
**Beer**, **wine**, and **traditional** **spirits** **served** at **weddings** and **festivals**, plus **imported** **brands** in **cities**. **Non**-**alcoholic** **options** **grew** **too**—**mocktails** and **zero**-**alcohol** **beer** for **drivers** and **health**-**conscious** **guests**.

---
#### 3. When do people usually drink alcohol?
**Evenings**, **weekends**, **holidays**, and **social** **gatherings**—**rarely** **before** **work** or **class** in **polite** **company**. **Business** **dinners** **sometimes** **include** **toasts**, but **pressure** to **drink** **varies** by **region** and **generation**.

---
#### 4. Do you think alcohol is dangerous?
**In** **excess**, **yes**—**addiction**, **accidents**, **violence**, and **long**-**term** **health** **damage** **follow** **heavy** **use**. **Moderate** **social** **drinking** **exists** **widely**, but **education** about **limits** and **designated** **drivers** **matters** **especially** for **young** **drivers**.

---
#### 5. Have you ever tried making alcoholic drinks?
**Not** **seriously**—**maybe** **helping** **stir** a **punch** **bowl** at a **party** or **watching** **relatives** **brew** **traditional** **drinks** at **home**. **Home** **distilling** **sounds** **risky** **without** **proper** **knowledge**, so I **wouldn't** **experiment** **blindly**.

---
#### 6. Is drinking alcohol common among young people in your country?
**In** **some** **circles**, **yes**—**clubs**, **student** **parties**, and **foreign**-**influenced** **nightlife** in **cities**. **Many** **young** **people** **abstain** for **religion**, **health**, **sport**, or **family** **rules**, so **patterns** **split** **sharply** **by** **background**.
