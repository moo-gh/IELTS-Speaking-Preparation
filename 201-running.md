#### 1. Do you like running?
**Moderately**—**short** **runs** **clear** my **head**, but I **don't** **chase** **marathon** **distances** or **competitive** **times**. **Bad** **weather** and **tight** **joints** **after** **long** **study** **days** **make** me **skip** it **more** **often** than I **plan**.

---
#### 2. How often do you go running?
**Maybe** **once** or **twice** a **week** when **schedule** and **energy** **align**, **less** during **exam** **season**. **Even** **fifteen** **minutes** **helps**, so I **try** not to **cancel** **completely** when I'm **busy**.

---
#### 3. Where do you usually run?
**Parks**, **riverside** **paths**, and **quiet** **streets** **near** **home**—**anywhere** with **fresh** **air** and **fewer** **cars**. **Treadmills** **work** in **winter**, but **outdoor** **runs** **feel** **less** **boring** for me.

---
#### 4. Did you run when you were at school?
**Yes**, during **PE** **classes**, **sports** **days**, and **occasional** **training** for **team** **events**. I **wasn't** the **fastest**, but **relay** **races** and **warm**-**up** **laps** **built** **basic** **stamina** I **still** **use** **now**.

---
#### 5. What are the benefits of running?
**Cardio** **fitness**, **stress** **relief**, **better** **sleep**, and a **simple** **routine** that **needs** **minimal** **equipment**. **Social** **runs** with **friends** **also** **motivate** me when **solo** **discipline** **fails**.

---
#### 6. Is running popular in your country?
**Yes**, **especially** in **cities** with **park** **networks** and **fun** **runs** for **charity**. **Apps** that **track** **steps** and **routes** **boosted** **interest**, though **many** **people** **prefer** **walking** or **gym** **work** **instead**.
