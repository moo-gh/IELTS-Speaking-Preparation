#### 1. Do you wear sunglasses?
**Yes**, **especially** in **bright** **sun**, when **driving**, or on **summer** **walks**. They **reduce** **glare** and **make** **outdoor** **time** **more** **comfortable**, **though** I **don't** **wear** them **indoors** or on **grey** **days**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever lost a pair of sunglasses?
**Once** or **twice**—**left** them on a **café** **table** or in a **taxi**. **Cheap** **pairs** are **easier** to **replace**, so I **try** not to **spend** **too** **much** unless I **really** **need** a **sturdy** **pair**.

---
#### 3. Do you buy expensive sunglasses?
**Not** **usually**—I **care** more about **comfort**, **UV** **protection**, and a **fit** that **stays** on my **face**. **Brand** **names** **matter** **less** to me than **lenses** that **actually** **protect** my **eyes**.

---
#### 4. When do people in your country wear sunglasses?
**Mostly** in **summer**, at the **beach**, while **driving**, or during **outdoor** **sports**. **Some** **people** also **wear** them as a **fashion** **accessory**, even when the **sun** **isn't** **especially** **strong**.

---
#### 5. Do you think sunglasses are important?
**For** **eye** **comfort** and **protection**, **yes**—**strong** **sunlight** can **cause** **strain** and **long**-**term** **damage**. As a **style** **item** they're **optional**, but as **practical** **gear** they're **worth** **having**.

---
#### 6. Would you wear sunglasses as a fashion item?
**Sometimes**—a **good** **pair** can **finish** an **outfit**, but I **won't** **wear** them just to **look** **cool** in **dim** **light**. **Function** **comes** **first**; **style** is a **bonus**.
