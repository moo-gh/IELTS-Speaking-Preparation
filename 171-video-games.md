#### 1. Do you play video games?
**Sometimes**, but **not** **every** **day**—**puzzle**, **strategy**, and **occasional** **multiplayer** **games** when I **need** a **break** from **study**. I **enjoy** them **in** **moderation**, but I **avoid** **marathon** **sessions** that **steal** **sleep** or **focus**.

---
#### 2. What kind of video games do you like?
**Story**-**driven** **games**, **puzzles**, **sports** **simulators**, and **light** **co**-**op** **games** with **friends** **online**. I **dislike** **overly** **violent** **titles** and **games** that **push** **endless** **paid** **upgrades** **before** you can **progress** **fairly**.

---
#### 3. How often do you play video games?
**Maybe** **once** or **twice** a **week**, **more** during **holidays** and **less** during **exam** **season**. **Short** **sessions** of **twenty** to **forty** **minutes** **work** **best** for me—**longer** **stretches** **leave** me **tired** and **guilty** **afterwards**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer playing alone or with others?
**Alone** for **relaxation** and **story** **games**; **with** **friends** for **competitive** or **team** **modes** when **everyone** **stays** **good**-**humoured**. **Toxic** **chat** **online** **ruins** the **fun**, so I **mute** **strangers** and **stick** to **people** I **trust**.

---
#### 5. Do you think video games are good for children?
**In** **balance**, **yes**—they **build** **problem**-**solving**, **hand**-**eye** **coordination**, and **sometimes** **teamwork**, but **time** **limits** and **content** **choices** **matter** **hugely**. **Without** **rules**, **games** can **replace** **sleep**, **exercise**, and **face**-**to**-**face** **play**.

---
#### 6. Has video gaming become more popular in your country?
**Definitely**—**mobile** **games**, **streaming**, **e**-**sports**, and **cheaper** **devices** **made** **gaming** **normal** across **age** **groups**, **not** **just** **teenagers**. **Parents** **debate** **screen** **time** **more** **openly** **now**, but **gaming** **culture** is **clearly** **mainstream**.
