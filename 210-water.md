#### 1. Do you drink a lot of water every day?
**I** **try** to—**around** **six** to **eight** **glasses**, **especially** after **exercise** or in **hot** **weather**. On **busy** **study** **days** I **sometimes** **forget** and **only** **notice** when I **feel** **tired** or **thirsty**.

---
#### 2. Do you prefer drinking tap water or bottled water?
**Tap** **water** when it's **safe** and **tastes** **fine**—it's **cheaper** and **creates** **less** **plastic** **waste**. I **use** **bottled** **water** mainly when **travelling** or if the **local** **supply** **doesn't** **taste** **good**.

---
#### 3. Is clean drinking water important in your country?
**Extremely**—**access** to **clean** **water** **affects** **health**, **cooking**, and **daily** **comfort**. **Cities** **usually** **manage** it **well**, but **some** **areas** still **face** **quality** or **shortage** **problems**.

---
#### 4. Do you like swimming or other water activities?
**Yes**—**swimming** in a **pool** is **relaxing**, and I **enjoy** **quiet** **time** near **lakes** or the **sea**. I'm **less** **confident** in **rough** **open** **water**, so I **stick** to **calmer** **places**.

---
#### 5. Have you ever experienced a water shortage?
**Once** or **twice**—**pipes** **under** **repair** or **hot** **summers** when **pressure** **dropped**. It **made** me **more** **careful** about **short** **showers** and **not** **wasting** **water** while **washing** **dishes**.

---
#### 6. Do you think people waste too much water?
**Many** **do**—**long** **showers**, **running** **taps**, and **over**-**watering** **gardens** **add** **up** **quickly**. **Small** **habits** like **fixing** **leaks** and **using** **full** **loads** in **machines** can **reduce** **waste** without much **effort**.
