#### 1. Do you like eating in restaurants?
**Yes**, **especially** when the **food** is **good**, the **place** is **clean**, and I **don't** have to **wash** **dishes** **afterwards**. **Quiet** **cafés** or **small** **family** **restaurants** **beat** **noisy** **chains** for me on **most** **days**.

---
#### 2. How often do you eat in restaurants?
**Not** **every** **week**—**maybe** **once** or **twice** a **month** for **meetings** with **friends**, **birthdays**, or **days** when **cooking** **feels** **too** **tiring**. **Exam** **season** I **eat** **out** **less** to **save** **money** and **time**.

---
#### 3. What kind of restaurants do you like?
**Simple** **places** with **fresh** **ingredients** and **reasonable** **prices**—**local** **dishes**, **grills**, or **Asian** **noodles** **depending** on my **mood**. I **avoid** **overly** **fancy** **menus** where I **spend** **half** the **meal** **reading** **descriptions** I **don't** **understand**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer eating at home or in restaurants?
**Home** for **daily** **life**—**healthier**, **cheaper**, and **easier** to **control** **portions**. **Restaurants** **win** for **celebrations**, **trying** **new** **flavours**, and **social** **time** when **everyone** **wants** to **relax** **together**.

---
#### 5. Have you ever worked in a restaurant?
**No**, **not** **properly**, though I **helped** at a **family** **event** **serving** **food** **once**. **Restaurant** **work** **looks** **stressful**—**long** **hours**, **hot** **kitchens**, and **demanding** **customers** **during** **peak** **times**.

---
#### 6. Are restaurants popular in your country?
**Very**—**cities** have **endless** **options**, from **street** **snacks** to **high**-**end** **dining**, and **young** **people** **meet** **friends** there **regularly**. **Delivery** **apps** **boosted** **popularity** **too**, so **eating** **out** or **ordering** **in** **feels** **normal** **now**.
