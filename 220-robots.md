#### 1. Are you interested in robots?
**Yes**, **to** **a** **point**—I **follow** **news** about **vacuum** **robots**, **factory** **machines**, and **AI** **assistants**, but I'm **not** a **tech** **enthusiast** who **builds** them **myself**. They **feel** **fascinating** because they **blend** **engineering** and **everyday** **life**.

---
#### 2. Would you like to have a robot at home?
**For** **simple** **chores** like **cleaning** or **reminding** me about **tasks**, **maybe**—it **could** **save** **time**. I **wouldn't** want one that **records** **everything** I **say** **without** **clear** **privacy** **controls**.

---
#### 3. What can robots do well today?
**Repetitive** **jobs**—**assembling** **parts** in **factories**, **sorting** **packages**, **mapping** **rooms**, and **assisting** in **hospitals** with **lifting** or **delivery**. They're **weaker** at **judgment**, **empathy**, and **handling** **unexpected** **situations** **alone**.

---
#### 4. Do you watch films or read stories about robots?
**Sometimes**—**sci**-**fi** **movies** about **androids** and **AI** are **popular**, and they **make** me **think** about **ethics** more than **gadgets**. I **prefer** **stories** that **focus** on **human** **choices**, not just **explosions**.

---
#### 5. Do you think robots will replace many human jobs?
**Some** **roles**, **yes**—especially **routine** **office** and **manual** **work**. **Skilled** **jobs** that **need** **creativity**, **negotiation**, and **real** **care** will **still** **need** **people**, though **workers** may **have** to **retrain**.

---
#### 6. Are robots common in your country?
**In** **industry** and **large** **shops**, **yes**—**automated** **lines**, **self**-**checkout**, and **delivery** **drones** **appear** more **often**. At **home**, **robot** **vacuums** are **growing**, but **most** **families** **still** **rely** on **ordinary** **appliances** and **human** **help**.
