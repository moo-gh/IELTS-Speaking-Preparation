#### 1. Do you stay in hotels very often?
**Not** **really**—**maybe** **once** or **twice** a **year** for **holidays**, **family** **events**, or **short** **trips** when **distance** **makes** **daily** **commuting** **impossible**. **Most** **weeks** I **sleep** at **home** or **stay** with **relatives** to **save** **money**.

---
#### 2. What do you think is important when choosing a hotel?
**Clean** **rooms**, **safe** **location**, **reliable** **Wi**-**Fi**, **fair** **price**, and **honest** **reviews** from **recent** **guests**. **Noise** **control** **matters** **too**—**thin** **walls** can **ruin** **sleep** **even** when **photos** **look** **perfect** **online**.

---
#### 3. Have you ever had a bad experience in a hotel?
**Yes**—**once** a **room** **smelled** **damp**, **another** **time** **check**-**in** **took** **forever** and **staff** **seemed** **indifferent**. **Small** **problems** **feel** **bigger** when you're **tired** **after** **travel** and **just** **want** to **rest**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer staying in hotels or with friends/family?
**With** **friends** or **family** when **possible**—**warmer**, **cheaper**, and **more** **personal**. **Hotels** **win** when I **need** **privacy**, **flexible** **hours**, or **no** **one** I **know** **lives** **nearby**.

---
#### 5. What services do you expect from a good hotel?
**Clean** **linen**, **hot** **water**, **helpful** **reception**, **clear** **billing**, and **basic** **breakfast** or **nearby** **food** **options**. **Extras** like **gym** or **pool** are **nice**, but **sleep** **quality** and **security** **come** **first**.

---
#### 6. Are hotels expensive in your country?
**In** **tourist** **cities**, **yes**—**prices** **jump** during **holidays** and **major** **events**. **Budget** **options** **exist**, but **decent** **mid**-**range** **hotels** can **still** **cost** **more** than **people** **expect**, **especially** for **students** and **young** **travellers**.
