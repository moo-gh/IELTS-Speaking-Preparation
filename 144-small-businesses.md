#### 1. Do you know many small businesses where you live?
**Yes**—**bakeries**, **corner** **shops**, **phone** **repair** **kiosks**, **cafés**, and **family**-**run** **restaurants** **line** the **streets** near **home**. They're **part** of **daily** **errands**, not **just** **tourist** **stops**.

---
#### 2. Do you prefer buying things from big companies or small businesses?
**Depends** on the **item**—**electronics** and **branded** **clothes** I **often** **buy** from **larger** **stores** for **warranty** and **choice**. For **bread**, **coffee**, **repairs**, and **quick** **gifts**, **small** **shops** **feel** **friendlier** and **sometimes** **cheaper**.

---
#### 3. Have you ever worked in a small business?
**Briefly**—I **helped** in a **relative's** **shop** during a **holiday**, **stocking** **shelves** and **serving** **customers**. It **taught** me how **fast** **busy** **hours** **hit** and how **much** **patience** **retail** **needs**.

---
#### 4. Have you ever thought about starting your own business?
**Occasionally**—a **small** **café** or **language**-**related** **service** **sounds** **appealing**, but I **know** **risk**, **capital**, and **consistency** **matter** more than a **nice** **idea**. For **now** I **focus** on **skills** and **experience** **first**.

---
#### 5. What kinds of small businesses are popular in your country?
**Food** **stalls**, **barbers**, **tailors**, **mobile** **repair** **shops**, **tutoring** **centres**, and **neighbourhood** **grocers** are **everywhere**. **Online**-**first** **micro** **businesses**—**homemade** **food**, **handicrafts**, **reselling**—have **grown** **fast** **too**.

---
#### 6. Do you think small businesses are important for a local community?
**Very**—they **create** **local** **jobs**, **keep** **money** **circulating** **nearby**, and **give** **areas** **character** beyond **identical** **chains**. When **they** **disappear**, **streets** **feel** **colder** and **less** **convenient** for **everyday** **needs**.
