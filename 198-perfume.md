#### 1. Do you like wearing perfume?
**Sometimes**, but **lightly**—**a** **subtle** **scent** on **special** **days** **feels** **nice**, **heavy** **sprays** **give** me **headaches** in **closed** **rooms**. I **prefer** **fresh**, **clean** **smells** **over** **sweet** **floral** **clouds** that **linger** **for** **hours**.

---
#### 2. Do you wear perfume often?
**Not** **daily**—**maybe** **meetings**, **events**, or **dates** when I **want** to **feel** **put** **together**. **Study** **days** I **skip** it **completely** because **libraries** and **exam** **halls** **punish** **strong** **fragrances** **fast**.

---
#### 3. What kind of perfume do you like?
**Citrus**, **woody**, or **light** **aquatic** **notes**—**nothing** **too** **mature** or **powdery**. **Unisex** **options** **appeal** to me **more** than **labels** **marketed** **only** with **gender** **stereotypes** on the **bottle**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer light or strong scents?
**Light**, **definitely**—**one** or **two** **sprays** **max**, **preferably** on **clothes** rather than **skin** if the **weather** is **hot**. **Strong** **scents** **feel** **aggressive** in **crowded** **buses** and **offices** where **others** **can't** **escape** them.

---
#### 5. Have you ever given perfume as a gift?
**Once** or **twice** for **relatives** when I **knew** their **taste** **well** **enough**—**otherwise** it's **risky** because **smell** is **personal**. **Gift** **sets** with **small** **samples** **work** **better** than **guessing** a **full**-**size** **bottle** **blindly**.

---
#### 6. Is perfume popular in your country?
**Yes**, **especially** in **cities**—**department** **stores**, **duty**-**free** **shops**, and **online** **influencers** **push** **brands** **constantly**. **Traditional** **attar**-**style** **oils** **still** **matter** **too**, **alongside** **global** **designer** **labels** at **formal** **events**.
