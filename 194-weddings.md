#### 1. Have you ever been to a wedding?
**Yes**, **several**—**relatives**, **family** **friends**, and **one** **university** **friend's** **ceremony** with a **small** **reception** **afterwards**. I **remember** the **music**, **food**, and **long** **photo** **sessions** **more** than **every** **formal** **speech**.

---
#### 2. What happens at a typical wedding in your country?
**Ceremony**, **family** **blessings**, **exchange** of **rings** or **traditional** **symbols**, then a **large** **meal** with **dancing** and **gifts**. **Some** **events** **last** **two** **days**, **mixing** **religious** **customs** with **modern** **party** **elements**.

---
#### 3. Do you like weddings?
**Mostly**, **yes**—**happy** **atmosphere**, **reunion** with **relatives**, and **excuse** to **dress** **formally** **without** **work** **pressure**. **Very** **long** **programs** **tire** me, **especially** when **seating** **plans** **separate** me from **close** **friends**.

---
#### 4. What clothes do people wear to weddings?
**Formal** **suits** or **traditional** **outfits** for **men**, **elegant** **dresses** or **cultural** **dresses** for **women**, **often** with **bright** **colours** **avoiding** **plain** **white** **unless** you're the **bride**. **Guests** **usually** **check** **dress** **codes** **carefully** to **avoid** **offending** **hosts**.

---
#### 5. Are weddings expensive in your country?
**Very**—**venues**, **catering**, **photography**, **decoration**, and **guest** **lists** **push** **costs** **high** **quickly**. **Families** **sometimes** **save** for **years**, and **young** **couples** **debate** **large** **traditional** **weddings** versus **smaller** **private** **celebrations**.

---
#### 6. Would you prefer a big wedding or a small one?
**Small** to **medium**—**close** **people**, **less** **stress**, **lower** **cost**, and **more** **time** to **talk** **properly** with **guests**. A **huge** **crowd** **looks** **impressive** in **photos**, but **intimacy** **matters** **more** to me **personally**.
