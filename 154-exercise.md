#### 1. Do you do much exercise?
**Some**, **not** **enough**—I **walk** **regularly** and **try** to **hit** the **gym** a **few** **times** a **week**, but **exam** **periods** **disrupt** the **habit** **fast**. **Even** **short** **sessions** **help** me **feel** **clearer** **after** **long** **study** **days**.

---
#### 2. What kind of exercise do you enjoy?
**Walking**, **light** **jogging**, and **occasional** **gym** **workouts**—**nothing** **too** **extreme**. **Team** **sports** **can** be **fun** with **friends**, but I **prefer** **flexible** **solo** **exercise** I can **fit** around my **schedule**.

---
#### 3. Did you do much exercise when you were a child?
**Yes**, **mostly** **outdoors**—**football** in **alleys**, **cycling**, **running** around with **friends** **without** calling it **exercise**. **School** **PE** **classes** **added** **structure**, but **play** **felt** **natural** then.

---
#### 4. Do you think it is important for people to exercise?
**Very**—**bodies** **need** **movement**, **stress** **drops**, and **sleep** **often** **improves** when you're **active** **regularly**. **Modern** **life** **sits** **too** **much**; **even** **modest** **habits** **beat** **doing** **nothing**.

---
#### 5. What are the benefits of regular exercise?
**Better** **energy**, **stronger** **focus**, **healthier** **mood**, and **less** **tension** in my **shoulders** after **hours** at a **desk**. **Long** **term**, it **supports** **heart** **health** and **helps** me **maintain** a **routine** **outside** **screens**.

---
#### 6. Where do you usually exercise?
**Nearby** **parks**, **local** **streets** for **walking**, and a **basic** **gym** when I **want** **equipment** and **weather** **turns** **bad**. **Home** **workouts** **happen** **sometimes**, but I **focus** **better** **away** from my **bed** and **phone**.
