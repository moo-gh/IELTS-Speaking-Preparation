#### 1. Do you like exams?
**Not** **really**—they **create** **pressure**, **narrow** **focus**, and **sometimes** **reward** **memorisation** **over** **real** **understanding**. I **accept** them as **part** of **education**, but I **rarely** **enjoy** the **weeks** **before** a **big** **test**.

---
#### 2. How do you prepare for exams?
**Start** **early**, **summarise** **notes**, **practice** **past** **papers**, and **sleep** **properly** instead of **cramming** **all** **night**. **Study** **groups** **help** when **everyone** **stays** **serious**; **solo** **revision** **works** **better** for **topics** I **already** **understand** **weakly**.

---
#### 3. What was the most difficult exam you have taken?
**Probably** a **major** **university**-**entrance** or **language** **test** where **timing** and **vocabulary** **both** **mattered** **heavily**. I **remember** **finishing** **exhausted**, **unsure** whether **small** **mistakes** would **cost** me **dearly**.

---
#### 4. Do exams make you nervous?
**Yes**, **especially** **before** the **paper** **starts**—**hands** **shake**, **mind** **goes** **blank** for a **second**, then **settles** **once** I **read** the **first** **question**. **Preparation** **reduces** **panic**, but **some** **nerves** **never** **fully** **disappear**.

---
#### 5. Are exams important?
**To** **some** **extent**, **yes**—they **measure** **progress**, **motivate** **revision**, and **open** **doors** to **schools** and **jobs**. **Still**, **one** **score** **shouldn't** **define** a **person** **completely**; **creativity**, **character**, and **life** **skills** **matter** **too**.

---
#### 6. How do people in your country feel about exams?
**Very** **seriously**—**families** **invest** **time** and **money** in **tutoring**, and **results** **shape** **reputation** **locally**. **Competition** can **drive** **achievement**, but it **also** **creates** **stress** for **students** who **fear** **falling** **behind** **peers**.
