#### 1. Do you admire any celebrities?
**Yes**, **mainly** **people** known for **skill** and **discipline**—**athletes**, **musicians**, **scientists** on **podcasts**, and **filmmakers** who **take** **creative** **risks**. I **admire** **talent**, but I **don't** **treat** **fame** **alone** as **proof** of **character**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever met a celebrity?
**Not** **really**—**maybe** a **local** **TV** **presenter** at an **event** from **a** **distance**, **nothing** like a **long** **conversation**. **Autograph** **queues** and **crowded** **concerts** are the **closest** I've **come** to **meeting** **anyone** **widely** **famous**.

---
#### 3. Do you follow any celebrities on social media?
**A** **few**, **mostly** for **updates** on **music**, **films**, or **causes** I **care** about—**not** **every** **post** they **share**. I **unfollow** **quickly** when **content** **feels** **fake**, **angry**, or **purely** **commercial** **without** **substance**.

---
#### 4. Would you like to be famous?
**No**, **not** **full**-**time** **fame**—**privacy** **matters** **too** **much**, and **online** **criticism** **looks** **exhausting**. **Recognition** for **meaningful** **work** **sounds** **appealing**, but **constant** **public** **attention** **doesn't**.

---
#### 5. Are celebrities good role models for young people?
**Some** **can** **be**, if they **promote** **hard** **work**, **kindness**, and **healthy** **habits** **openly**. **Many** **aren't** **realistic** **models** because **wealth**, **staff**, and **edited** **images** **hide** how **ordinary** **life** **actually** **works**.

---
#### 6. Why are celebrities popular in your country?
**Entertainment** **culture**, **social** **media**, **reality** **shows**, and **national** **pride** when **local** **stars** **succeed** **abroad** **all** **fuel** **interest**. **People** **follow** **celebrities** for **escapism**, **style** **ideas**, and **conversation** **topics** as **much** as for **talent** **itself**.
