#### 1. Do you like going to coffee shops?
**Yes**, **especially** when the **atmosphere** is **calm**, the **Wi**-**Fi** **works**, and I **don't** **feel** **rushed** to **leave** **immediately**. **Good** **coffee** **helps**, but **comfortable** **seating** and **reasonable** **noise** **levels** **matter** **just** as **much**.

---
#### 2. How often do you go to coffee shops?
**Maybe** **once** or **twice** a **week**—**more** during **exam** **season** when I **want** a **change** of **scene**, **less** when **money** is **tight**. **Weekends** I **go** **socially** with **friends** **more** than for **serious** **study**.

---
#### 3. What do you usually order in a coffee shop?
**Cappuccino** or **latte** in **winter**, **iced** **coffee** or **cold** **brew** in **summer**, **sometimes** **tea** if **caffeine** **already** **feels** **too** **high**. I **rarely** **order** **fancy** **seasonal** **drinks** unless **someone** **else** **suggests** **sharing** **one**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer studying in coffee shops or at home?
**Home** for **deep** **focus**—**fewer** **distractions** and **no** **pressure** to **keep** **buying** **drinks**. **Coffee** **shops** **win** for **light** **reading**, **planning**, or **short** **writing** **sessions** when I **need** **background** **energy** from **other** **people** **working** **nearby**.

---
#### 5. Are coffee shops popular in your country?
**Very**, **especially** in **cities**—**international** **chains**, **local** **roasters**, and **small** **independent** **cafés** **compete** for **students**, **remote** **workers**, and **meeting** **culture**. **Instagram**-**friendly** **interiors** **boosted** **popularity** **too**, **not** **just** the **coffee** **itself**.

---
#### 6. What makes a good coffee shop?
**Fair** **prices**, **clean** **tables**, **polite** **staff**, **reliable** **internet**, **enough** **power** **outlets**, and **music** **quiet** **enough** to **think**. **Crowded** **shops** with **nowhere** to **sit** **quickly** **lose** my **patience**, **no** **matter** how **good** the **espresso** **tastes**.
