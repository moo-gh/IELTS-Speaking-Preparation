#### 1. Do you often lose things?
**Not** **constantly**, but **enough** to **annoy** me—**earphones**, **receipts**, and **occasionally** my **keys** when I'm **rushed**. I **lose** **track** of **small** **items** **more** than **big** **ones** because they **slip** into **pockets** and **bags** **without** **notice**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever lost something important?
**Yes**—I **misplaced** my **student** **ID** before an **exam** **week** and **spent** an **hour** **retracing** my **steps** until I **found** it **inside** a **jacket** **pocket**. **Losing** **important** **documents** **teaches** you to **keep** **copies** and **check** **bags** **before** **leaving**.

---
#### 3. What kinds of things do people often lose?
**Keys**, **phones**, **wallets**, **umbrellas**, **transport** **cards**, and **small** **accessories** like **earbuds** or **glasses**. **Busy** **days** and **multitasking** **make** it **easy** to **put** something **down** and **forget** **where**.

---

**Pause**, **breathe**, and **retrace** my **route** **slowly** instead of **panicking** **immediately**. I **check** **recent** **photos**, **call** **places** I **visited**, and **use** **phone** **tracking** for **devices** when **possible**.

---
#### 5. Why do people lose things?
**Distraction**, **stress**, **carrying** **too** **much**, and **habit**—**same** **spot** **every** **day** until **one** **day** you **don't**. **Routines** **break** during **travel** or **deadline** **weeks**, and **small** **objects** **simply** **blend** into **background** **clutter**.

---
#### 6. Have you ever found something that someone else lost?
**A** **few** **times**—**student** **cards**, **gloves**, and **once** a **phone** on a **bus** **seat**. I **handed** them to **staff** or **posted** in **local** **groups** because **losing** something **valuable** **feels** **awful**, and **returning** it **takes** **minutes**.
