#### 1. Do you like fishing?
**I've** **tried** it a **few** **times**, but I'm **not** a **serious** **angler**. I **enjoy** the **quiet** and being **outdoors**, though **sitting** for **hours** without a **catch** can **feel** **slow**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever gone fishing?
**Yes**—**once** by a **lake** with **relatives** and **once** near the **coast** on a **holiday**. I **caught** **very** **little**, but the **trip** was still **relaxing** and **good** for **conversation**.

---
#### 3. Is fishing popular in your country?
**In** **some** **regions**, **yes**—**especially** near **rivers**, **lakes**, and the **sea**. **Many** **people** treat it as a **weekend** **hobby**, while **others** **fish** for **food** or **small** **business**.

---
#### 4. Would you like to go fishing more often?
**Occasionally**, **yes**—for **fresh** **air** and a **break** from **screens**. I **wouldn't** make it a **weekly** **habit** unless I **lived** **closer** to a **good** **spot** and had more **free** **time**.

---
#### 5. What do people need for fishing?
**Basic** **gear** like a **rod**, **line**, **hooks**, and **bait**, plus a **licence** in **many** **places**. **Patience**, **weather** **awareness**, and **respect** for **local** **rules** **matter** as **much** as the **equipment**.

---
#### 6. Do you think fishing is a good hobby?
**For** **people** who **like** **nature** and **slow** **activities**, **yes**—it can **reduce** **stress** and **encourage** **outdoor** **time**. It **isn't** **ideal** for **everyone**, **especially** if they **prefer** **fast**, **social** **sports**.
