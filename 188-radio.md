#### 1. Do you listen to the radio?
**Sometimes**, **mainly** in **cars** or **when** **someone** **else** **turns** it on at **home**—I **don't** **tune** in **daily** like my **parents** **did**. **Streaming** and **podcasts** **replaced** **radio** for **me** **most** of the **time**, but **live** **radio** **still** **feels** **useful** for **local** **news**.

---
#### 2. What kind of programs do you like on the radio?
**News** **briefings**, **traffic** **updates**, **short** **interviews**, and **occasional** **music** **blocks** **without** **too** **much** **talk** between **songs**. I **dislike** **long** **argument** **shows** that **raise** **voices** **for** **drama** **instead** of **clarity**.

---
#### 3. When do you usually listen to the radio?
**During** **short** **drives**, **while** **cooking**, or **in** the **background** when **studying** **light** **tasks**. **Morning** **shows** **catch** me **if** I **wake** up **early** and **want** **weather** and **headlines** **without** **opening** my **phone**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer radio or TV?
**TV** for **films** and **visual** **stories**; **radio** when I **only** **need** **sound** and **don't** **want** to **stare** at a **screen**. **Radio** **feels** **less** **demanding**, which **helps** when I'm **tired** or **multitasking**.

---
#### 5. Is radio popular in your country?
**Yes**, **especially** among **drivers**, **shop** **owners**, and **older** **listeners** who **trust** **familiar** **presenters**. **Younger** **people** **stream** **more**, but **radio** **remains** **strong** for **live** **sports**, **emergency** **alerts**, and **regional** **talk**.

---
#### 6. Did you listen to the radio when you were a child?
**Yes**—**family** **trips**, **kitchen** **background** **noise**, and **occasional** **children's** **segments** on **weekend** **mornings**. I **remember** **request** **shows** and **contest** **calls** **more** than **specific** **presenters**, but **radio** **felt** **normal** **everywhere**.
