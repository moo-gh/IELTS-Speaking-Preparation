#### 1. Do you like ice cream?
**Yes**, **especially** in **summer**—**cold** **dessert** **feels** like an **instant** **reward** after a **long** **hot** **day**. I **don't** **eat** it **daily**, but I **enjoy** it **enough** to **say** **yes** **without** **thinking**.

---
#### 2. Did you like ice cream when you were a child?
**Absolutely**—it **meant** **birthdays**, **park** **trips**, and **bribing** **myself** to **finish** **homework**. **Parents** **limited** it **mostly** for **health** **reasons**, which **somehow** made it **taste** **even** **better**.

---
#### 3. What is your favourite flavour?
**Pistachio** or **dark** **chocolate**—**not** **too** **sweet**, with a **clear** **flavour** you can **actually** **taste**. **Plain** **vanilla** **works** **too** when the **quality** is **good**, but **artificial** **rainbow** **colours** **put** me **off**.

---
#### 4. Are there any ice cream shops near where you live?
**Several**—**local** **dessert** **shops**, **supermarket** **freezers**, and a **few** **international** **chains** **near** **busy** **streets**. In **summer** **evenings** the **queues** **outside** **popular** **spots** **get** **long** **fast**.

---
#### 5. How often do you eat ice cream?
**Maybe** **once** or **twice** a **month** in **normal** **weeks**, **more** often on **holidays** or when **friends** **suggest** **dessert** **after** **dinner**. **Winter** I **eat** it **less**, unless someone **buys** a **tub** for **movie** **nights** at **home**.

---
#### 6. Can you make ice cream yourself?
**Not** **properly**—I've **tried** **simple** **blended** **frozen** **banana** **versions**, but **real** **ice** **cream** **needs** **patience**, **freezer** **space**, and **better** **equipment** than I **own**. For **special** **occasions** I **buy** it **instead** of **experimenting** in the **kitchen**.
