#### 1. Do you often feel lonely?
**Sometimes**, **not** **constantly**—**busy** **weeks** with **classes** and **friends** **mask** it, but **quiet** **evenings** or **being** **new** in a **city** can **hit** **harder** than I **expect**. **Social** **media** **connects** me **superficially** while **real** **conversation** **still** **feels** **missing**.

---
#### 2. When do you feel lonely?
**After** **moving** for **study**, **when** **close** **friends** **travel**, or **during** **long** **exam** **periods** when **everyone** **cancels** **plans**. **Holidays** can **feel** **lonely** **too** if **family** is **far** away and ** routines** **break** **suddenly**.

---
#### 3. What do you do when you feel lonely?
**Message** someone I **trust**, **walk** **outside**, **cook** **something** **simple**, or **join** a **study** **group** **instead** of **isolating** **completely**. **Passive** **scrolling** **often** **worsens** the **feeling**, so I **try** to **switch** to **small** **real** **actions**.

---
#### 4. Is loneliness a problem for young people in your country?
**Increasingly**, **yes**—**competition**, **online** **life**, and **unstable** **housing** **make** **connection** **harder** even when **people** **look** **busy** **online**. **Universities** **talk** about **mental** **health** **more**, but **stigma** **still** **stops** **some** **students** from **asking** for **help**.

---
#### 5. Do you prefer spending time alone or with others?
**Balance**—**alone** **time** **recharges** me for **deep** **work**, but **too** **much** **solitude** **drags** my **mood** **down**. **Small** **groups** **beat** **huge** **crowds** for me on **most** **days**.

---
#### 6. How can people deal with loneliness?
**Reach** **out** **early**, **join** **clubs**, **volunteer**, **maintain** **routines**, and **limit** **comparison** on **social** **media**. **Professional** **support** **matters** when **loneliness** **lasts** **weeks** and **affects** **sleep**, **appetite**, or **motivation** **seriously**.
