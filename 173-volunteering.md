#### 1. Have you ever done volunteer work?
**Yes**, **a** **few** **times**—**helping** at **school** **events**, **community** **clean**-**ups**, and **one** **short** **project** **supporting** **local** **students** with **study** **materials**. **Nothing** **long**-**term** **yet**, but those **experiences** **showed** me how **small** **effort** can **matter** **locally**.

---
#### 2. Would you like to do volunteer work in the future?
**Definitely**—**especially** **roles** tied to **education**, **environment**, or **helping** **newcomers** **settle** in a **city**. I'd **prefer** **structured** **projects** with **clear** **goals** rather than **showing** up **once** and **disappearing** **afterwards**.

---
#### 3. What kind of volunteer work could you do?
**Tutoring**, **language** **practice** with **beginners**, **organising** **books** or **supplies**, and **occasional** **event** **support** where **planning** **skills** **help**. I'm **less** **suited** to **medical** **volunteering**, but **practical** **community** **tasks** **fit** my **strengths** **better**.

---
#### 4. Is volunteering popular in your country?
**Growing**, **especially** among **students** who **want** **experience** and **references** **before** **graduation**. **Religious** **groups**, **NGOs**, and **universities** **organise** **projects** **regularly**, though **many** **people** **still** **volunteer** **informally** through **family** and **neighbours**.

---
#### 5. Why do people volunteer?
**To** **help** **others**, **build** **skills**, **meet** **like**-**minded** **people**, and **feel** **useful** **beyond** **paid** **work**. **Some** **also** **volunteer** for **career** **networking**, but **genuine** **motivation** **usually** **mixes** **personal** **growth** with **social** **responsibility**.

---
#### 6. Do you think volunteering is important?
**Yes**—**communities** **run** on **more** than **money**; **time**, **care**, and **shared** **effort** **fill** **gaps** that **institutions** **miss**. **Volunteering** **also** **teaches** **empathy** and **reminds** **people** that **problems** **nearby** **deserve** **attention** **too**.
