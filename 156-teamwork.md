#### 1. Have you worked in a team before?
**Yes**, **often** at **school** and **university**—**group** **projects**, **presentations**, and **occasional** **volunteer** **tasks** where **roles** **had** to be **shared**. **Some** **teams** **flowed** **smoothly**; **others** **needed** **clear** **deadlines** to **avoid** **last**-**minute** **panic**.

---
#### 2. Do you prefer working alone or in a team?
**It** **depends** on the **task**—**solo** **work** **suits** **writing**, **revision**, and **deep** **focus**; **teams** **help** when **ideas**, **skills**, and **energy** **need** to be **combined**. I **dislike** **groups** where **one** **person** **does** **everything** while **others** **vanish**.

---
#### 3. What makes a good team?
**Clear** **goals**, **honest** **communication**, **fair** **work** **sharing**, and **people** who **respect** **deadlines** **without** **drama**. **Good** **teams** **also** **listen** when **someone** **struggles** instead of **blaming** them **publicly**.

---
#### 4. What are the advantages of teamwork?
**Better** **ideas**, **shared** **pressure**, **faster** **problem**-**solving**, and **learning** from **people** with **different** **strengths**. **Socially**, it **builds** **trust** and **teaches** you how to **negotiate** **without** **turning** **small** **issues** into **conflicts**.

---
#### 5. Have you ever had difficulties working in a team?
**Yes**—**unequal** **effort**, **conflicting** **schedules**, and **vague** **instructions** **caused** **stress** **before** **exams**. We **fixed** it **partly** by **splitting** **tasks** **in** **writing** and **setting** **check**-**in** **times** **every** **few** **days**.

---
#### 6. Is teamwork important at school or work?
**Very**—**most** **real** **projects** **involve** **other** **people**, even in **fields** that **look** **individual** on **paper**. **School** **teams** **prepare** you for **workplace** **cooperation**, **feedback**, and **handling** **disagreement** **professionally**.
