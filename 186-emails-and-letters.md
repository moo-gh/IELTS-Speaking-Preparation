#### 1. Do you often write emails?
**Yes**, **almost** **daily**—**university** **applications**, **teacher** **questions**, **group** **project** **updates**, and **formal** **requests** to **offices** or **banks**. **Casual** **chat** **moved** to **messages**, but **email** **still** **handles** **anything** that **needs** a **record**.

---
#### 2. Do you prefer emails or letters?
**Emails** for **speed**, **attachments**, and **easy** **editing** **before** I **send**. **Letters** **feel** **more** **personal** for **thank**-**you** **notes** or **special** **occasions**, but **postage** and **waiting** **make** them **impractical** for **daily** **life**.

---
#### 3. When was the last time you wrote a letter?
**Months** **ago**, **maybe** a **birthday** **card** with **a** **short** **handwritten** **message** inside—**not** a **full** **formal** **letter** on **plain** **paper**. **True** **letter** **writing** **happens** **rarely** **now**, **except** for **official** **forms** or **family** **traditions**.

---
#### 4. Do you think email is useful?
**Extremely**—**documents**, **schedules**, and **clear** **instructions** **travel** **instantly**, which **saves** **time** for **study** and **work**. **Misused** **inboxes** **become** **stressful**, so **filters** and **short** **subject** **lines** **matter** **a** **lot**.

---
#### 5. Do you receive many emails every day?
**Enough** to **check** **regularly**—**newsletters**, **promotions**, **class** **notices**, and **occasional** **spam** **mixed** with **important** **messages**. I **try** to **unsubscribe** from **noise** so **real** **tasks** **don't** **get** **buried**.

---
#### 6. Is letter writing still common in your country?
**Not** for **everyday** **communication**—**most** **people** **use** **phones** and **email** **instead**. **Handwritten** **letters** **survive** for **formal** **invitations**, **condolences**, **some** **legal** **notices**, and **sentimental** **gifts** between **close** **relatives**.
