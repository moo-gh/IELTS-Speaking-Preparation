#### 1. How many hours do you sleep every day?
**Usually** around **seven** to **eight** **hours** on **good** **nights**, but **exam** **weeks** can **shrink** that to **six** if I **panic**-**revise** **late**. **Weekends** I **sometimes** **sleep** **longer** to **catch** **up**, **though** **irregular** **hours** **make** **Monday** **mornings** **harder**.

---
#### 2. Do you think you get enough sleep?
**Most** **days**, **yes**, but **not** **always** **quality** **sleep**—I **wake** **once** or **twice** if I'm **stressed** or **using** my **phone** **too** **late**. **Enough** **hours** on **paper** **doesn't** **guarantee** I **feel** **rested** the **next** **morning**.

---
#### 3. Do you take naps during the day?
**Rarely**—**short** **naps** **help** **sometimes**, but **longer** **ones** **mess** up my **night** **sleep** and **leave** me **groggy**. If I **nap** at **all**, I **set** an **alarm** for **fifteen** or **twenty** **minutes** **max**.

---
#### 4. Do you have a fixed bedtime?
**I** **try** to, **around** **eleven** on **weeknights**, but **deadlines**, **messages**, and **"just** **one** **more** **video"** **break** the **routine**. **Consistency** **works** **best** when I **treat** **bedtime** like a **real** **appointment**, **not** a **suggestion**.

---
#### 5. What do you usually do before going to bed?
**Shower**, **dim** the **lights**, **pack** my **bag** for the **next** **day**, and **read** **something** **light** instead of **scrolling** **social** **media**. **Calmer** **habits** **help** me **fall** **asleep** **faster** than **staring** at a **bright** **screen** in **bed**.

---
#### 6. Do you think sleep is important?
**Absolutely**—**memory**, **mood**, **focus**, and even **immune** **health** **depend** on **rest** more than **people** **admit**. **Sacrificing** **sleep** for **extra** **study** **often** **backfires** because I **work** **slower** and **make** **more** **mistakes** the **next** **day**.
