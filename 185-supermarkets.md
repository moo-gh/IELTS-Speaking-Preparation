#### 1. How often do you go to the supermarket?
**Once** or **twice** a **week**, **sometimes** **more** if **flatmates** and I **share** **cooking** and **run** **out** of **basics** **fast**. **Before** **exams** I **shop** **less** **often** and **rely** on **simple** **meals** **until** **deadlines** **pass**.

---
#### 2. Do you like shopping in supermarkets?
**It's** **fine**, but **not** **fun**—**bright** **lights**, **long** **aisles**, and **weekend** **crowds** **wear** me **down** **quickly**. I **prefer** **getting** **in**, **finding** **what** I **need**, and **leaving** **without** **wandering** **aimlessly**.

---
#### 3. What do you usually buy at the supermarket?
**Fruit**, **vegetables**, **bread**, **milk**, **eggs**, **rice** or **pasta**, **snacks**, and **cleaning** **supplies** when **stock** **runs** **low**. **Frozen** **food** **helps** on **busy** **days**, though I **try** not to **fill** the **basket** with **only** **processed** **items**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer supermarkets or small local shops?
**Local** **shops** for **fresh** **bread**, **herbs**, or **quick** **top**-**ups** **near** **home**; **supermarkets** for **bulk** **buying** and **better** **prices** on **household** **essentials**. **Convenience** **stores** **win** **late** at **night**, but **supermarkets** **win** for **weekly** **planning**.

---
#### 5. Has supermarket shopping changed in recent years?
**Yes**—**self**-**checkout**, **delivery** **apps**, **loyalty** **apps**, and **more** **ready**-**made** **meals** **changed** how **people** **shop**. **Online** **orders** **grew** **a** **lot**, **especially** after **people** **got** **used** to **doorstep** **delivery**.

---
#### 6. Do you make a shopping list before you go to the supermarket?
**Usually**, **yes**—**a** **short** **list** on my **phone** **stops** me **buying** **random** **snacks** I **don't** **need**. I **still** **forget** **one** **item** **sometimes**, which **means** a **second** **trip** or **borrowing** from **flatmates** **the** **next** **day**.
