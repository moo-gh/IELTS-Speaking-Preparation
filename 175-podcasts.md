#### 1. Do you listen to podcasts?
**Yes**, **fairly** **often**—**language** **practice**, **news** **summaries**, **interviews**, and **occasional** **story** **series** when I'm **commuting** or **doing** **simple** **tasks** at **home**. They're **easy** to **pause**, which **fits** my **schedule** **better** than **long** **TV** **episodes**.

---
#### 2. What kind of podcasts do you like?
**Education**, **science** **explained** **simply**, **personal** **development**, and **light** **comedy** when I **need** a **break** from **heavy** **topics**. I **avoid** **overly** **long** **rambling** **shows** unless the **host** **keeps** **ideas** **clear** and **structured**.

---
#### 3. When do you usually listen to podcasts?
**On** **buses**, **while** **walking**, **cooking**, or **before** **sleep** if the **topic** **isn't** **too** **stimulating**. **Morning** **commutes** **work** **best** for **learning** **content**; **evenings** **suit** **relaxed** **conversation** **shows** **more**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer podcasts or radio?
**Podcasts**, **mainly** because I **choose** the **episode** and **resume** **exactly** where I **stopped**. **Radio** **feels** **better** for **live** **events** and **local** **news**, but **podcasts** **win** for **focused** **learning** on **demand**.

---
#### 5. Would you like to make your own podcast?
**Maybe** **one** **day** about **study** **tips** or **language** **learning**, but **not** **yet**—**recording**, **editing**, and **staying** **consistent** **sound** **harder** than **just** **listening**. I'd **need** a **clear** **format** and **enough** **free** **time** to **prepare** **properly**.

---
#### 6. Are podcasts popular in your country?
**Increasingly**, **especially** among **students** and **commuters** who **want** **hands**-**free** **content**. **Global** **platforms** **dominate**, but **local** **creators** **cover** **politics**, **culture**, and **business** **too**, **often** in **mix** of **languages**.
