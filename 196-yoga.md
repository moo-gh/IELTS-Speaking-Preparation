#### 1. Have you ever tried yoga?
**Yes**, **a** **few** **times**—**beginner** **classes** at **university** and **short** **sessions** from **online** **videos** when my **back** **felt** **tight** from **sitting** **too** **long**. I'm **not** **advanced**, but **basic** **stretches** **already** **helped** my **posture** **somewhat**.

---
#### 2. Do you think yoga is popular in your country?
**Growing**, **especially** in **cities**—**studios**, **gym** **classes**, and **apps** **target** **office** **workers** and **students** who **want** **flexibility** **without** **heavy** **weights**. **Older** **generations** **sometimes** **view** it as **foreign**, but **younger** **people** **try** it **openly**.

---
#### 3. What are the benefits of yoga?
**Flexibility**, **balance**, **calmer** **breathing**, **better** **posture**, and **mental** **focus** when **practised** **consistently**. It **won't** **replace** **cardio** **completely**, but it **complements** **running** or **gym** **work** **nicely** for **recovery** and **stress**.

---
#### 4. Would you like to learn yoga in the future?
**Yes**, **properly**—**guided** **classes** would **stop** me **guessing** **poses** and **risking** **injury** from **bad** **form**. **Even** **twenty** **minutes** **several** **times** a **week** **sounds** **realistic** if I **build** it into my **routine**.

---
#### 5. Do you prefer yoga or other forms of exercise?
**Other** **forms** for **energy** and **strength**—**walking**, **swimming**, or **light** **gym** **sessions** **fit** my **personality** **better** **day**-**to**-**day**. **Yoga** **wins** when I **feel** **stiff**, **anxious**, or **too** **tired** for **intense** **workouts**.

---
#### 6. Where do people usually do yoga?
**Studios**, **gyms**, **parks** in **mild** **weather**, and **living** **rooms** via **online** **classes** **after** **work**. **Group** **classes** **motivate** **beginners**; **home** **practice** **suits** **people** with **busy** **schedules** who **already** **know** the **basics**.
