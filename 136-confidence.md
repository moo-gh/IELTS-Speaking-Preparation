#### 1. Would you describe yourself as a confident person?
**Moderately**, **yes**—I **speak** **up** in **classes** I **know** **well**, but **new** **social** **settings** **still** **make** me **overthink** **first** **impressions**. **Confidence** **grows** with **preparation**, not **pure** **personality** **alone**.

---
#### 2. Were you a confident child?
**Outgoing** with **friends**, **quieter** with **strangers**—**classroom** **reading** **aloud** **terrified** me **until** **practice** **helped**. **Sports** **with** **mates** **felt** **easy**; **formal** **presentations** **took** **years** to **tame**.

---
#### 3. What made you nervous as a child at school?
**Speaking** in **front** of the **class**, **strict** **teachers** **calling** **names** **randomly**, and **fear** of **getting** **answers** **wrong** **publicly**. **Tests** **printed** in **tiny** **font** **somehow** **felt** **scarier** than the **questions** **themselves**.

---
#### 4. Can you tell by looking at someone whether they are confident or not?
**Sometimes**—**posture**, **eye** **contact**, and **steady** **voice** **hint** at it, but **people** **fake** **confidence** **well** **too**. **Quiet** **listeners** **aren't** **always** **insecure**; **loud** **talkers** **aren't** **always** **secure**.

---
#### 5. Are men usually more confident than women?
**Stereotypes** **suggest** that, but **real** **life** **varies** **hugely** by **personality**, **culture**, and **experience**. **Some** **women** **lead** **rooms** **effortlessly**; **some** **men** **avoid** **spotlight** **despite** **expectations**.

---
#### 6. What do you do to help you build confidence?
**Prepare** **thoroughly**, **rehearse** **out** **loud**, **ask** for **feedback**, and **celebrate** **small** **wins** instead of **comparing** myself to **others** **online**. **Exercise** and **sleep** **surprisingly** **help** **before** **big** **days**.
