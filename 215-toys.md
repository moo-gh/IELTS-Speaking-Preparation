#### 1. Did you play with toys when you were a child?
**Yes**—I had **cars**, **building** **blocks**, and a **few** **action** **figures**. I **spent** **hours** on the **floor** **making** **stories**, and those **toys** still **bring** back **clear** **memories**.

---
#### 2. What was your favourite toy?
A **set** of **colourful** **building** **blocks**—I could **rebuild** them **every** **day** into **something** **new**. **Unlike** **battery** **toys**, they **never** got **boring** and **encouraged** me to be **creative**.

---
#### 3. Do you think toys are important for children?
**Definitely**—they **support** **imagination**, **motor** **skills**, and **social** **play** with **friends** or **siblings**. **Simple** **toys** can **teach** **sharing** and **problem**-**solving** better than **expensive** **gadgets**.

---
#### 4. Do adults play with toys in your country?
**Some** **do**—**collectors**, **board**-**game** **fans**, and **people** who **enjoy** **puzzles** or **model**-**making**. It is **less** **common** than in **childhood**, but **hobbies** like that are **widely** **accepted**.

---
#### 5. Have toys changed compared with the past?
**Yes**—**many** are now **electronic**, **app**-**connected**, or based on **popular** **films**. **Traditional** **toys** still **exist**, but **screens** and **online** **games** take up more of **children's** **play** **time**.

---
#### 6. Would you buy toys as gifts for children?
**Often**, **yes**—if I **know** the **child's** **age** and **interests**. I **prefer** **safe**, **educational**, or **creative** **toys** over **noisy** **ones** that **break** after a **week**.
