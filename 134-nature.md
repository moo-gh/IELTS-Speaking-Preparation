#### 1. Do you spend much time in nature?
**Some**, **not** **enough**—**weekend** **walks**, **short** **trips** to **mountains** or **coastlines**, and **occasional** **picnics** when **weather** **cooperates**. **City** **weeks** **trap** me **indoors** over **screens**, so I **push** for **green** **breaks** **deliberately**.

---
#### 2. Do you have many plants in your home?
**A** **few** **pots**—**mint**, **basil**, and **one** **hardy** **indoor** **plant** that **survives** **neglect**. **Real** **gardens** **need** **space** we **don't** **have**, but **balcony** **greenery** **helps** **mood** **noticeably**.

---
#### 3. Have you ever grown vegetables?
**Briefly**—**tomatoes** and **herbs** on a **balcony**; **pests** and **irregular** **watering** **limited** the **harvest**. **Grandparents** **grew** **more** **seriously** in **villages**; I **mostly** **helped** as a **child** rather than **leading** **projects**.

**Yes**—**basic** **recycling**, **pollution**, **water** **saving**, and **simple** **science** **trips** to **parks** or **museums**. **Depth** **varied** by **teacher**; **some** **classes** **felt** **inspiring**, **others** **just** **memorise**-**and**-**forget**.

---

**Definitely**—**fresh** **air**, **movement**, and **unstructured** **play** **beat** **hours** **glued** to **tablets**. **Safety** and **urban** **design** **matter** **too**; **parents** **need** **clean**, **accessible** **spaces**, not **just** **lectures** about **screens**.
