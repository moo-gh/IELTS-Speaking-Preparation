#### 1. Do you like fast food?
**Yes**, **honestly**—**burgers**, **fried** **chicken**, **pizza**, and **quick** **noodles** **hit** the **spot** when I'm **hungry** and **short** on **time**. I **enjoy** the **taste**, but I **know** it's **not** something I **should** **rely** on **every** **day**.

---
#### 2. How often do you eat fast food?
**Maybe** **once** or **twice** a **month** during **normal** **weeks**, **slightly** **more** when **deadlines** **stack** and **cooking** **feels** **impossible**. **Exam** **season** is when I'm **most** **tempted**, **especially** **late** at **night**.

---
#### 3. What fast food do you like?
**Grilled** **options** when **available**, **classic** **burgers**, **wraps**, and **occasional** **Asian** **fast** **food** like **noodle** **boxes** or **rice** **dishes**. I **try** to **avoid** **giant** **meal** **deals** that **leave** me **sluggish** **afterwards**.

---
#### 4. Is fast food popular in your country?
**Extremely**—**global** **chains**, **local** **brands**, and **delivery** **apps** **made** it **part** of **daily** **life** in **cities**. **Young** **people** **eat** it **socially** **too**, **not** **just** when they're **in** a **rush**.

---
#### 5. Do you think fast food is healthy?
**Mostly** **no** if you **eat** it **often**—**high** **salt**, **fat**, **sugar**, and **large** **portions** **add** up **quickly**. **Occasional** **meals** are **fine**, but **regular** **fast** **food** **makes** **energy** and **weight** **harder** to **manage**.

---
#### 6. Do you prefer fast food or home-cooked meals?
**Home**-**cooked** for **daily** **life**—**healthier**, **cheaper**, and **more** **flexible** for my **taste**. **Fast** **food** **wins** when I'm **out** with **friends**, **travelling**, or **too** **tired** to **shop** and **cook** **properly**.
