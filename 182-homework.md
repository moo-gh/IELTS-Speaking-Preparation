#### 1. Do you like doing homework?
**Not** **much**—it **eats** **evening** **time** and **sometimes** **feels** like **repetition** of **class** **work** **without** **enough** **feedback**. **Still**, when **tasks** are **clear** and **useful**, I **don't** **mind** them **as** **much**.

---
#### 2. How much homework do you get every day?
**It** **varies**—**light** **days** might be **an** **hour**, **heavy** **weeks** **closer** to **three** **hours** with **essays**, **readings**, and **problem** **sets** **stacked** **together**. **Group** **projects** **stretch** the **load** **even** **longer** **near** **deadlines**.

---
#### 3. When do you usually do your homework?
**Late** **afternoon** or **evening** after **classes**, **sometimes** **split** into **blocks** with **short** **breaks**. If I **delay** **too** **long**, I **end** up **rushing** **after** **midnight**, which **hurts** **quality** and **sleep**.

---
#### 4. Did you get much homework when you were younger?
**Less** than **now**, but **still** **enough**—**math** **drills**, **spelling**, and **simple** **reading** **tasks** **most** **weeknights**. **Parents** **checked** **more** **closely** **then**, so I **finished** **earlier** **even** when I **wanted** to **play** **outside**.

---
#### 5. Do you think homework is important?
**To** **some** **degree**, **yes**—it **reinforces** **ideas**, **builds** **discipline**, and **shows** **teachers** where **students** **struggle**. **Too** **much** **busywork** **backfires**, **especially** when **students** **copy** **answers** **without** **understanding**.

---
#### 6. What do you do if you don't understand your homework?
**Re**-**read** **notes**, **search** for **examples**, **ask** **classmates**, or **message** the **teacher** if **deadlines** **allow**. **Leaving** **confusion** **until** the **last** **minute** **almost** **always** **makes** **stress** **worse** than the **original** **question**.
