#### 1. Do you like gardening?
**Yes**, **in** **small** **doses**—**watering** **plants**, **clearing** **weeds**, and **watching** **something** **grow** **feels** **calming** after **long** **hours** at a **screen**. I **wouldn't** call myself an **expert**, but I **enjoy** the **routine** when **weather** **cooperates**.
