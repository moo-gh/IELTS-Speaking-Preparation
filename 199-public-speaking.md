#### 1. Do you like public speaking?
**Not** **naturally**—my **stomach** **tightens** **before** **big** **audiences**, even when I **know** the **topic** **well**. **Small** **class** **presentations** **feel** **manageable**; **formal** **stages** **still** **intimidate** me **somewhat**.

---
#### 2. Have you ever spoken in public?
**Yes**, **many** **times** at **school** and **university**—**project** **defences**, **debates**, **awards**, and **one** **community** **event** where I **introduced** a **guest** **speaker**. **Each** **experience** **taught** me that **preparation** **beats** **talent** on **bad** **nights**.

---
#### 3. What makes public speaking difficult?
**Nerves**, **fear** of **blanking**, **judging** **eyes**, **bad** **acoustics**, and **slides** that **fail** **mid**-**talk**. **Time** **limits** **stress** me **too**—I **either** **rush** or **over**-**explain** until the **moderator** **signals** me to **stop**.

---
#### 4. How can people improve their public speaking skills?
**Practice** **out** **loud**, **record** **yourself**, **learn** **clear** **structure**, **breathe** **slowly**, and **start** with **friendly** **audiences** **before** **large** **crowds**. **Feedback** from **teachers** or **peers** **shortens** **bad** **habits** **faster** than **silent** **rehearsal** **alone**.

---
#### 5. Is public speaking important?
**Very**—**education**, **work**, **interviews**, and **leadership** **all** **require** **explaining** **ideas** **clearly** **under** **pressure**. **Even** **introverts** **benefit** from **basic** **confidence**, because **hiding** **forever** **limits** **opportunities** **quietly**.

---
#### 6. Did you do presentations at school?
**Regularly**—**group** **posters**, **PowerPoint** **talks**, **language** **oral** **exams**, and **occasional** **competitions**. **Early** **presentations** were **embarrassing**, but they **built** **muscle** **memory** for **standing** up **later** in **university** and **job** **settings**.
