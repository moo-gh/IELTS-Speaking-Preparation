#### 1. Do you like going to parties?
**Yes**, **when** the **crowd** is **friendly** and I **know** at **least** a **few** **people** **well** **enough** to **relax**. **Huge** **noisy** **events** **drain** me **fast**, but **small** **celebrations** with **music** and **food** **usually** **feel** **worth** going to.

---
#### 2. What kind of parties do people in your country have?
**Birthday** **parties**, **wedding** **celebrations**, **graduation** **gatherings**, **holiday** **parties**, and **informal** **house** **parties** among **friends**. **Older** **relatives** **often** **host** **family** **meals** that **feel** like **parties** **even** **without** **dancing**.

---
#### 3. When was the last time you went to a party?
**A** **friend's** **birthday** **gathering** **a** **few** **weeks** **ago**—**cake**, **photos**, **light** **music**, and **staying** **until** **midnight** **chatting** in the **kitchen**. **Nothing** **fancy**, but **good** **company** **made** it **memorable**.

---
#### 4. Do you prefer big parties or small gatherings?
**Small** **gatherings**—**easier** to **talk**, **less** **pressure** to **perform**, and **simpler** to **leave** when I'm **tired**. **Big** **parties** can be **exciting** **once** in a **while**, but they're **not** my **default** **choice**.

---
#### 5. What do you usually do at parties?
**Chat**, **eat**, **take** **photos**, **help** with **serving** **food**, and **occasionally** **dance** if the **mood** **feels** **right**. I **rarely** **stay** at the **centre** of **attention**—I **prefer** **circulating** and **catching** up with **different** **groups**.

---
#### 6. Are parties important in your culture?
**Yes**—they **mark** **life** **events**, **strengthen** **family** **ties**, and **give** **people** a **formal** **reason** to **celebrate** **together**. **Even** **modest** **parties** **matter** because **shared** **memories** **last** **longer** than **gifts** **alone**.
